Hotel room renovation cost in Ghana is best planned per key — the cost of bringing one guest room, with its bathroom, to the standard you want. Owners in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi and Tamale usually ask for one number per room. The honest answer is a range set by star level, room size, bathroom scope and how many rooms share one design. This guide sets out those drivers, what Ghana Tourism Authority grading covers in general terms, and how to renovate while keeping the hotel open.

## What "per key" includes in a hotel room renovation

Before comparing quotes, agree what one key covers. Contractors in Ghana often quote different scopes under the same label.

| Package | Typically includes |
|---|---|
| Soft refresh | Paint, curtains, bedding, lamps, minor repairs, some loose furniture |
| Room renovation | New flooring, headboard wall, wardrobe and desk joinery, lighting, doors, loose furniture |
| Room and bathroom renovation | All of the above plus new sanitaryware, tiling, vanity, shower enclosure and mirror |
| Full refurbishment | All of the above plus MEP upgrades (air conditioning, power, data, plumbing), acoustic work and ceilings |

Each step up adds trades, time on site and coordination. The bathroom is usually the most expensive part of a room. It also causes the most delay.

## Per-key cost drivers by star level

We do not publish a single per-key price, because room sizes and scope vary too much between hotels. Instead, use this table to see what changes as you move up the star levels.

| Cost driver | 1–2 star | 3 star | 4–5 star |
|---|---|---|---|
| Room size and layout | Compact; standard layout | Moderate; some room types | Larger rooms, suites, bespoke layouts |
| Bathroom | Shower, basic sanitaryware | Shower or bath, mid-range fittings, vanity | Separate shower and bath in some rooms, premium fittings, stone or large-format tile |
| Joinery | Simple wardrobe and desk | Built-in wardrobe, headboard panel, luggage rack | Full millwork walls, minibar units, concealed storage |
| Lighting | Functional ceiling and bedside | Layered lighting, reading lights | Scene control, decorative and feature lighting |
| Doors | Standard room doors | Better acoustic and fire performance | Fire-rated, acoustic doors with electronic locks |
| Soft furnishings | Basic curtains and bedding | Blackout curtains, upholstered pieces | Custom fabrics, rugs, artwork |
| MEP | Repairs only | Air-conditioning and power upgrades | Full MEP, room controls, data |

As a component reference, our [Ghana villa renovation budget 2026](/blog/ghana-villa-renovation-budget-2026) prices sanitaryware per bathroom at $280–$490 (standard), $560–$1,050 (mid-range) and $1,400–$3,500 (premium), and engineered internal doors at $105–$280 each. Hotel specifications for fire rating, acoustics and locks differ from villas, so treat these as a rough reference, not a hotel quote.

## What GTA grading requires, in general terms

Hotels in Ghana are registered and licensed by the Ghana Tourism Authority under the Tourism Act, 2011 (Act 817). The accommodation regulations (L.I. 2239) group establishments into categories. Hotels can be star-classified from one to five stars.

In general terms:

- **Registration and licensing:** the premises must be registered, and the business licensed before it provides accommodation, with renewal when the licence expires.
- **Classification by inspection:** for star grading, the Authority assesses the property, with inspectors reporting against the schedule for the requested level.
- **Rising standards per star:** each level adds requirements covering room size, bathrooms, furnishings, lobby and reception, food and beverage, recreation and staffing.
- **Operating standards:** fire safety, security, hygiene, lighting, air conditioning and utilities are all part of the assessment.

If a renovation is meant to support a regrade, check the current requirements with GTA before design is fixed. Room sizes and bathroom provisions are expensive to change once tiling and joinery are installed.

## Phased renovation while the hotel keeps trading

Few owners can close a hotel for months. Phasing keeps revenue coming in, but it changes how materials are ordered and delivered.

| Approach | How it works | What it adds to cost |
|---|---|---|
| Floor by floor | Close one floor at a time; guests use the rest | Dust and noise protection, after-hours noisy work |
| Wing or block | Close a whole wing; easier site control | Fewer phases, but more rooms offline at once |
| Room batches | Renovate small groups of rooms in rotation | More set-ups, slower progress, tight logistics |
| Mock-up room first | Finish one room fully before ordering the rest | Small delay up front; avoids repeating mistakes across all keys |

A mock-up room is the single most useful step. It tests the layout, finishes, lighting and sanitaryware with real guests and staff before every key is ordered. For imported FF&E, a phased programme can mean several shipments. Consolidating each phase into full containers keeps freight and Tema clearance costs under control. Our villa breakdown gives $1,750–$3,150 for a 40-ft container from China and $1,050–$2,100 for port and customs handling at Tema per project.

Under the ECOWAS CET, duty bands are 0%, 5%, 10%, 20% and 35% by HS code. VAT 15%, NHIL 2.5% and GETFund 2.5% follow on CIF plus duty. Confirm classifications with a licensed customs broker before pricing.

## FF&E planning: the list that keeps the budget honest

FF&E (furniture, fixtures and equipment) is where hotel budgets drift, because items get added one at a time. Build the full list per room type before asking for prices: beds and headboards, wardrobes, desks, chairs, luggage racks, minibar units, lighting, curtains, mirrors, safes, artwork and bathroom accessories. Then multiply by keys, and add spares for the items guests damage most often, such as lamps, chairs and bathroom accessories. Our [hotel FF&E checklist](/guides/hotel-ffe-checklist) sets this out room by room.

For electrical items, specify 230V / 50Hz. Type G sockets are the common standard in Ghana, so imported lamps, kettles and safes should come with the right plugs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to renovate a hotel room in Ghana?

It depends on scope and star level. A soft refresh costs far less than a full room-and-bathroom renovation with MEP upgrades. Send your room count, sizes and target star level for a per-key range.

### Is it cheaper to renovate a hotel in phases or all at once?

Closing fully is usually cheaper on construction, because there is less protection and sequencing. Phasing costs more to build but keeps revenue coming in. Compare the lost room nights against the extra site cost.

### Which costs the most in a hotel room renovation?

Usually the bathroom: sanitaryware, tiling, waterproofing and plumbing. Joinery and MEP upgrades come next.

### Do I need GTA approval to renovate my hotel?

Hotels must be registered and licensed by the Ghana Tourism Authority, and star grading follows inspection. If your renovation affects classification, check current requirements with GTA before design is fixed.

### How long does a hotel room renovation take in Ghana?

It depends on the number of keys, phasing and whether FF&E is imported. Build in time for a mock-up room and for production and shipping of imported items before rooms close.
