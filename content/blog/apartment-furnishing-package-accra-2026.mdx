An apartment furnishing package in Accra lets an owner, investor or developer furnish a flat in one order instead of buying piece by piece. In 2026 the main questions are what a 1-, 2- or 3-bed package should contain, how a short-let specification differs from a home you will live in, and how developers furnish many identical units. This guide sets out package contents, compares the options, and explains the cost drivers. Use it to compare quotes on equal terms.

## What a 1-, 2- and 3-bed package includes

Package contents should be listed room by room. This is a typical baseline; layouts and scope vary.

| Room / area | 1-bed | 2-bed | 3-bed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Living | Sofa, coffee table, TV unit, side table, rug | As 1-bed, larger sofa or extra armchair | As 2-bed, sectional or two sofas |
| Dining | Table for 2–4, chairs | Table for 4, chairs | Table for 6, chairs, sideboard |
| Master bedroom | Bed, mattress, two bedside tables, wardrobe, dressing table | Same | Same, larger wardrobe |
| Other bedrooms | — | One bedroom: bed, mattress, bedside table, wardrobe | Two bedrooms: same, plus study desk option |
| Kitchen accessories | Cookware, crockery, cutlery, small appliances | Same, scaled up | Same, scaled up |
| Lighting | Ceiling and table lamps | Same, more points | Same, more points |
| Soft furnishings | Curtains, bedding, cushions | Same, per room | Same, per room |
| Bathroom accessories | Mirror, towel rails, shelves | Per bathroom | Per bathroom |
| Balcony (optional) | Two chairs, small table | Same | Same or larger set |

Fixed items such as kitchen cabinets, wardrobes built into walls, internal doors and sanitaryware are often quoted separately from loose furniture. If you need both, ask for one schedule so nothing falls between two quotes.

## Short-let vs owner-occupied: how the specification changes

Short-let apartments in areas such as East Legon, Airport Residential, Cantonments and Osu are judged in guest reviews and photos. Owner-occupied homes are judged by the people who live in them for years.

| Factor | Short-let / serviced | Owner-occupied |
|---|---|---|
| Fabrics | Performance, stain-resistant, removable covers | Personal choice; natural fabrics acceptable |
| Furniture finish | Scratch-resistant laminate, sealed edges | Veneers, lacquer, solid wood |
| Mattresses | Mid-firm, standard sizes, protector included | Personal preference |
| Replaceability | Standard items you can re-order | One-off pieces are fine |
| Storage | Lockable owner's cupboard, luggage space | Full wardrobes, more storage |
| Kitchen kit | Complete, matched sets for the guest count | Owner brings own items |
| Styling | Neutral, photographs well | Personal |
| Electrical | All items 230V / 50Hz, Type G plugs | Same |

The short-let rule: spend on mattresses, sofas and lighting, which guests notice, and keep loose items standard, so a broken chair can be replaced without restyling the room.

## Developer bulk packs: furnishing many units at once

Developers furnishing a block of apartments, or offering "furnished" as a sales option, gain most from a standard pack per unit type. One specification for each of the 1-, 2- and 3-bed types means one set of drawings, one production run and one installation method.

| Bulk-pack decision | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Fix unit types early | Packs follow floor plans; late layout changes mean re-ordering |
| Offer two or three tiers | Lets buyers upgrade without custom one-offs |
| Pack and label by unit | Pallets marked by unit number save days on site |
| Phase deliveries to handover | Avoids storing furniture in an unfinished building |
| Hold spare stock | Covers transit damage without waiting for a re-order |
| Combine with fixed items | Kitchens, wardrobes, doors and sanitaryware in the same shipment |

Our villa breakdown reports a 2–5% damage rate per container, with replacements taking 6–10 weeks, and recommends 5% buffer stock. That advice applies equally to apartment packs.

## What drives the cost of a furnishing package

| Cost driver | Pushes cost up | Keeps cost down |
|---|---|---|
| Unit size and bedroom count | More rooms, larger living areas | Compact layouts |
| Tier | Premium fabrics, solid wood, designer lighting | Functional laminate and standard fabrics |
| Mattresses | Pocket-spring, hybrid, premium foams | Standard foam or bonnell spring |
| Kitchen and bathroom kit | Branded appliances, full sets | Essentials only |
| Fixed joinery | Built-in wardrobes, TV walls, custom units | Freestanding pieces |
| Quantity | One unit, one-off items | Many identical units |
| Logistics | Small shipments, upcountry delivery | Full containers, delivery to Accra |
| Installation | Assembly on upper floors without a lift | Assembly with lift access, unit-labelled pallets |

## Timing the package with handover

Order furniture once the floor plan and finishes are fixed, and schedule delivery for when the unit is clean, painted and secure. Our building materials guide gives 8–12 weeks from order to site for imported goods, so order around the time internal finishes start.

Before the furniture arrives, check that lift access, door widths and stair turns allow large items such as sofas and wardrobes to reach every unit. Agree who unpacks, assembles and removes packaging, and where waste goes. For short-let units, add a final snag walk with the property manager, so missing items are listed before the first guest checks in rather than after a poor review.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to furnish an apartment in Accra in 2026?

It depends on bedroom count, tier and whether fixed joinery is included. The Decoropic estimator gives a range for apartments by size and tier; for a priced schedule, send your floor plan.

### Is it cheaper to buy a furnishing package or buy furniture piece by piece?

A package usually saves on logistics, installation and time, because everything is specified, shipped and fitted together. Piece-by-piece buying gives more choice, but costs more in time and deliveries.

### Which is better for a short-let apartment: imported or locally made furniture?

Both can work. What matters is durability, easy cleaning and being able to replace items quickly. Compare the same specification on fabrics, finishes and mattresses.

### What should a 2-bedroom furnishing package include?

Living and dining furniture, two bedrooms with beds, mattresses, bedside tables and wardrobes, kitchen accessories, lighting, curtains, bedding and bathroom accessories. Confirm whether fixed joinery is included.

### Can developers order furnishing packs for a whole apartment block?

Yes. Standard packs per unit type, labelled by unit and delivered in phases to match handover, are the most efficient way to furnish many units.
