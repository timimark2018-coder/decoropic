The aluminium windows price in Ghana in 2026 is set by three choices: the profile series, the glass, and whether the frames are fabricated locally or imported as a complete system. Two quotes for "aluminium windows" on the same house can describe very different products. This guide explains the main options, compares local fabrication with imported systems, and breaks down the cost drivers per square metre, so you can compare quotes on equal terms.

## Profile series: what the numbers on a quote mean

Suppliers describe aluminium frames by "series", usually a number linked to the frame depth. A deeper profile is generally stiffer and can carry larger, heavier glass. In practice, the series alone does not tell you the quality. Ask for the profile wall thickness, the alloy and temper, the surface finish and the hardware brand.

| Profile type | Typical use | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Light sliding profiles | Small bedroom and bathroom windows, budget projects | Wall thickness, roller quality, rattle and water tightness |
| Standard casement / sliding systems | Most residential windows and balcony doors | Gaskets, drainage slots, multi-point locks |
| Heavy-duty sliding / lift-slide | Large patio openings, hotel and villa terraces | Track design, roller load rating, threshold height |
| Thermally broken systems | Air-conditioned rooms where cooling cost matters | Polyamide break, matched glass, sealing |
| Folding / bi-fold systems | Wide openings to gardens or pools | Hinge and track quality, alignment, security |

Finish matters on the coast. Powder coating and anodising both protect aluminium, but salt air near the sea is hard on cheap coatings and plain steel screws. Specify a marine-grade or coastal finish and stainless or coated fixings for Accra and Tema sites.

## Glass options and their effect on cost

Glass is often a third or more of what you see on a window, and it is the part most quotes leave vague. Common choices:

- **Single clear glass:** lowest cost, no solar control, more heat gain.
- **Tinted or reflective glass:** reduces glare and some heat gain; popular on west-facing facades.
- **Tempered (toughened) glass:** stronger and safer when broken; needed for doors, low-level glazing and large panes.
- **Laminated glass:** holds together when broken; good for security and noise.
- **Insulated glass units (double glazing):** better for air-conditioned rooms and noise, and heavier, so they need a stronger profile.
- **Low-E coatings:** reduce heat entering the room; worth considering where cooling runs all day.

The right glass depends on orientation, room use and safety rules, not one choice for the whole house. A west-facing living room and a north-facing bathroom do not need the same unit.

When you compare quotes, ask each supplier to state the glass in the same format: thickness, type (clear, tinted, tempered, laminated or insulated), and any coating. A quote that only says "glass included" cannot be compared with one that specifies tempered tinted glass. The same applies to mosquito screens and burglar-proofing, which many Ghanaian homeowners add as standard but which are often priced as extras.

## Local fabrication vs imported systems

Ghana has many aluminium fabricators who buy profiles and glass and assemble on site or in a workshop. Imported systems arrive as finished, factory-made units or cut-to-size kits with matched hardware.

| Factor | Local fabrication | Imported system (factory-made) |
|---|---|---|
| Profiles | Whatever stock is available locally; varies by supplier | Specified series and wall thickness, consistent across the order |
| Tolerances | Depend on workshop tools and skill | Factory cutting and crimping; tighter, repeatable joints |
| Hardware | Mixed sources | Matched handles, locks, rollers and gaskets |
| Glass | Cut locally | Supplied with the frames, often pre-glazed |
| Site adjustment | Easy to change sizes late | Sizes must be final before production |
| Lead time | Quick start | Production, shipping and clearance; plan ahead |
| Best for | Small jobs, repairs, simple openings | Whole houses, apartment blocks, hotels, large openings |

The deciding point is usually scale and openings. For a few standard windows, local fabrication is practical. For a full villa, a block of identical apartments or a hotel where every window must match, imported systems reduce variation.

## Cost drivers per m²

When comparing quotes per m², make sure each quote covers the same items.

| Cost driver | Raises cost per m² | Lowers cost per m² |
|---|---|---|
| Opening type | Casement, tilt-turn, lift-slide, folding | Fixed panes, simple sliders |
| Unit size | Very small units (more frame per m²) or very large panes | Mid-size, standard modules |
| Profile | Heavier series, thermal break | Standard residential series |
| Glass | Laminated, insulated units, Low-E | Single tempered or clear |
| Finish | Wood-grain, special colours, marine-grade | Standard powder-coat colours |
| Hardware | Multi-point locks, premium brands | Standard handles and locks |
| Extras | Mosquito screens, burglar-proofing, sills | Bare frame and glass |
| Installation | Out-of-square openings, high-level work | Openings prepared to size |

For imported systems, add landed cost. Under the ECOWAS CET, Ghana's duty bands are 0%, 5%, 10%, 20% and 35%, set by HS code. VAT 15%, NHIL 2.5% and GETFund 2.5% are charged on CIF plus duty, and other levies follow. Our building materials guide puts the combined figure at roughly 20–35% of CIF for finished building materials; confirm your classification with a licensed customs broker.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do aluminium windows cost per m² in Ghana in 2026?

Our 2026 villa breakdown prices aluminium windows at $56–$140 per m². Profile series, glass, opening type and hardware decide where your windows land.

### Is it cheaper to fabricate aluminium windows locally or import them?

For a few simple windows, local fabrication is often practical. For a full house or block, imported systems can be competitive once consistency, hardware and re-work are counted. Compare both on the same profile, glass and hardware specification.

### Which glass is best for windows in Ghana?

It depends on orientation and room use. Tinted or Low-E glass helps on sun-facing facades; tempered or laminated glass is needed for doors and large panes; insulated units suit air-conditioned rooms.

### Which is better for Ghana, aluminium or uPVC windows?

Both are used. Aluminium is strong, slim and suits large openings; on the coast it needs a good finish. Compare warranty terms, hardware and local service support before choosing.

### How long do imported aluminium windows take to arrive in Accra?

Plan for 8–12 weeks from order to site. Sizes must be final before production, so measure finished openings first.
