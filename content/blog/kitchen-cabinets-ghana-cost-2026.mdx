Kitchen cabinets cost in Ghana in 2026 depends less on the door colour you choose and more on where the cabinets are built. Homeowners in Accra, Tema and Kumasi usually weigh three routes: factory-made cabinets imported from China, a local carpenter, or a European-brand kitchen through a showroom. Each route behaves differently in coastal humidity, against termites and on lead time. This guide compares them side by side and shows what actually moves the price.

## Imported vs local carpentry vs European brands: side-by-side

There is no single "best" route. Local carpentry is flexible and quick to adjust on site. Imported factory cabinets give consistent finishes and hardware at scale. European brands offer design pedigree and long-established systems, at a much higher entry point.

| Factor | Imported (factory, China) | Local carpentry (Ghana) | European brand (showroom) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical materials | Melamine-faced MDF or particleboard, moisture-resistant boards, plywood; lacquer, PET or laminate doors | Local hardwood, plywood or MDF bought locally; spray or hand finishes | High-pressure laminate, lacquer, veneer on engineered boards |
| Humidity performance | Good when boards are moisture-resistant and all edges are banded at the factory | Varies with timber drying and edge sealing; unseasoned timber can warp | Generally good; systems are engineered for wet areas |
| Termite risk | Low for engineered boards; any solid timber parts still need treatment | Higher with untreated solid timber; ask what treatment is used | Low for engineered boards |
| Hardware | Soft-close hinges and runners usually included; brand depends on spec | Depends on what the carpenter can source locally | Branded hardware as standard |
| Lead time | Production plus sea freight and Tema clearance; plan ahead | Shortest start time; finish time depends on the workshop | Longest, with ordering through a dealer and shipping |
| Warranty | Set out in the supply contract; confirm in writing | Usually informal; agree terms before work starts | Brand warranty through the dealer; check local service cover |
| What's included | Carcasses, doors, hardware, sometimes worktops and sinks; installation separate unless agreed | Build and fit together; materials often bought as you go | Cabinetry; installation and appliances often priced separately |

The honest trade-off: a good local carpenter with seasoned, treated timber can produce a fine kitchen. The risk is consistency, especially when the job runs to many units or several apartments. Factory production makes every door the same size and finish, which is why developers often import.

## What drives the cost of a kitchen in Ghana

Two kitchens with the same footprint can differ by several thousand dollars. These are the levers that move the number most.

| Cost driver | What pushes it up | What keeps it down |
|---|---|---|
| Linear metres and tall units | Long runs, full-height larders, islands | Straight or L-shaped runs, fewer tall units |
| Carcass board | Plywood, premium moisture-resistant board | Melamine-faced MDF with full edge banding |
| Door finish | High-gloss lacquer, veneer, profiled doors | Flat laminate or PET doors |
| Hardware | Premium runners, lift-up systems, internal organisers | Standard soft-close hinges and runners |
| Countertop | Natural stone, large slabs, many cut-outs | Quartz or compact surfaces in standard sizes |
| Appliances | Integrated, imported premium brands | Freestanding appliances, bought locally |
| Site conditions | Uneven walls, re-routed plumbing and power | Walls prepared and services fixed before measuring |
| Logistics | Shipping a kitchen on its own | Consolidating with doors, sanitaryware and lighting |

Electrical detail is easy to miss. Ghana runs on 230V / 50Hz, and Type G (three-pin) sockets are the common standard, with Type D also found. Imported appliances and under-cabinet lighting should be specified for 230V / 50Hz from the start, so nothing needs converters later.

## Duties, taxes and landed cost for imported cabinets

Imported kitchens are taxed when they clear at Tema. Under the ECOWAS Common External Tariff, Ghana applies duty bands of 0%, 5%, 10%, 20% and 35%. Finished consumer goods commonly fall in the 20% band, but the exact rate is set by the HS code your cabinets are declared under. VAT is then charged at 15%, with NHIL at 2.5% and GETFund at 2.5%, on a base that includes CIF value and duty. Other levies and charges are added on top.

In our building materials guide we estimate the combined duty and tax on finished building materials at roughly 20–35% of CIF. Treat that as a planning figure only: rates are fixed at clearance, so confirm with a licensed customs broker before you price your kitchen.

Port, freight and inland haulage also count. In our villa breakdown, port and customs handling at Tema runs $1,050–$2,100 per project, and Tema-to-Accra haulage $210–$420. A kitchen shipped alone carries all of that by itself. Shared with the rest of the house, it carries only part.

## Lead times and how to plan the kitchen into the build

Kitchen cabinets should be ordered only after walls are plastered, floors are screeded and plumbing and electrical points are fixed. Measuring earlier invites gaps, and re-made pieces cost far more than a week's wait.

A workable sequence:

1. Fix the layout and appliance list.
2. Finish walls, screed and services.
3. Site measurement and final drawings.
4. Production and pre-shipment inspection.
5. Sea freight, Tema clearance, delivery.
6. Installation, worktop templating, then worktop fitting.

Our building materials guide gives 8–12 weeks from order to site for imported materials, so order around the time plastering starts. Local carpentry starts sooner, but ask the workshop for a firm completion date and stage payments tied to delivery.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a kitchen cost in Ghana in 2026?

In our 2026 villa breakdown, a mid-range kitchen (cabinetry, countertop and appliances) for a 300 m² villa runs $8k–$15k. Cabinetry alone ranges from $3k–$5.5k in MDF/melamine to $5.5k–$10.5k in plywood/lacquer. Your layout and appliance list decide where you land.

### Is it cheaper to import kitchen cabinets from China or use a local carpenter?

It depends on scale and specification. Factory imports usually win on finish consistency and hardware for larger kitchens or several units. Local carpentry can be competitive for a small, simple kitchen. Compare both on the same specification, including duty and logistics for the import.

### Which is better for Ghana's humidity: MDF, plywood or solid wood?

Moisture-resistant board with full factory edge banding performs well. Plywood is stronger and handles moisture better than standard MDF. Solid timber works if it is properly seasoned and treated against termites.

### What import duty applies to kitchen cabinets in Ghana?

Duty follows the HS code under the ECOWAS CET bands (0–35%), then VAT 15%, NHIL 2.5% and GETFund 2.5%. Confirm the classification with a licensed customs broker.

### How long does it take to get imported kitchen cabinets to Accra?

Plan for 8–12 weeks from order to site, covering production, inspection, sea freight and Tema clearance. Measure only once walls and services are finished.
