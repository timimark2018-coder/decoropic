## The brief

A newly built four-bedroom house in Airport Hills with a modern, minimal exterior and a shell interior. The owner wanted the inside to match the architecture — clean lines, a feature wall in the living room, a kitchen with a separate walk-in pantry — but within a Mid-Range budget. The approach was to spend on the few surfaces people see and touch most (living-room feature wall, kitchen, bathrooms) and keep the rest to well-made standard ranges.

## Challenges and how we handled them

- **Feature-wall panels.** Wall panels are heavy and easily damaged at the edges. They were crated individually in Foshan and the installers in Accra were briefed on handling before the panels arrived.
- **Pantry fit.** Typical for a walk-in pantry, the room is small and every unit must fit first time. The pantry was re-measured after plastering and the cabinet run adjusted in the production drawings.
- **Mid-Range budget discipline.** Upgrading a few surfaces can quietly push the total up. The itemised budget tracked each upgrade against a Functional baseline so the owner could see the cost of each choice.

## Result

A modern four-bedroom house finished in 11 weeks with a living-room feature wall, a kitchen with walk-in pantry, four fitted bedrooms and three bathrooms.
