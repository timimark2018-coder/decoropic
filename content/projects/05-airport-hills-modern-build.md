## The brief

A newly built four-bedroom house in Airport Hills with a modern, minimal exterior and a shell interior. The owner wanted the inside to match the architecture — clean lines, a stone-look feature wall in the living room, a kitchen with a separate walk-in pantry — but within a Mid-Range budget. The approach was to spend on the few surfaces people see and touch most (living-room feature wall, kitchen fronts, bathroom tiles) and keep the rest to well-made standard ranges.

## What we supplied

- **Kitchen** — custom kitchen with laminate fronts, quartz-composite worktop and a fitted walk-in pantry with open shelving and drawer units.
- **Wardrobes** — fitted wardrobes in four bedrooms, melamine finish, sliding doors in the master.
- **Interior doors** — flush veneered doors with concealed frames for a clean modern line.
- **Sanitaryware** — wall-hung WCs, walk-in showers with glass screens, wall-mounted vanities in three bathrooms.
- **Hardware** — matt-black handles, hinges and bathroom accessories throughout.
- **Tiles & flooring** — 800 × 800 mm matt porcelain in living areas, matching skirting, anti-slip porcelain on terraces.
- **Lighting** — recessed downlights, LED strip under kitchen wall units and in the pantry, exterior downlights.

## Challenges and how we handled them

- **Large-format panels.** Travertine-effect panels are heavy and chip easily at the edges. They were crated individually in Foshan and the tilers in Accra were briefed on handling and adhesive before the panels arrived.
- **Pantry fit.** Typical for a walk-in pantry, the room is small and every unit must fit first time. The pantry was re-measured after plastering and the cabinet run adjusted in the production drawings.
- **Mid-Range budget discipline.** Upgrading a few surfaces can quietly push the total up. The itemised budget tracked each upgrade against a Functional baseline so the owner could see the cost of each choice.

## Result

A modern four-bedroom house finished in 11 weeks with a travertine-effect living-room feature wall, a kitchen with walk-in pantry, four fitted bedrooms and three bathrooms.
