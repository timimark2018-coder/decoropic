Public areas set a hotel's room rate before guests ever see a bedroom. This 2026 hospitality proposal coordinates four high-traffic zones — the double-height reception lobby, tropical lounge, buffet restaurant and bar — into a single Foshan millwork, stone, lighting and contract-seating package.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Location | Accra, Greater Accra, Ghana |
| Zones covered | Main reception lobby, tropical lounge, buffet dining hall & bar |
| Tier | Mid-Range to Premium Hospitality ($95–$150+ / m² planning range) |
| Key items | Custom stone reception desk, feature chandelier, contract lounge sofas, buffet island millwork, bar counter, bar stools & commercial dining chairs |
| Lead time | 10–14 weeks from shop-drawing sign-off to Tema arrival |

## Zone-by-zone highlights

- **Reception & tropical lounge:** High-wear porcelain floor slabs, acoustic timber wall slats, bespoke reception millwork and breathable commercial-grade lounge seating suited to Accra's climate.

![Hotel tropical lounge with commercial seating and aluminium glazing](/projects/11-accra-hotel-lobby-restaurant/tropical-lounge.webp)

- **Buffet dining & bar:** Custom sintered-stone buffet counter built for hot chafing dishes and heavy daily cleaning, paired with an illuminated feature bar and contract dining chairs.
