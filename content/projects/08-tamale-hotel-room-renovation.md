A boutique hotel in Tamale asked us to bring its standard rooms up to four-star level without changing the building. Room 105 is the sample room: 22 m² of bedroom and a 6.6 m² bathroom with no shower. We redesigned it in a Transitional American style and specified it to the Ghana Tourism Authority four-star requirements. The other five rooms on the floor will follow the same design.

## The brief

The rooms were wide but tired. The bathroom had only a WC and a basin, which rules out a four-star grading. The owner wanted a calm, upscale room that guests remember, built from durable finishes that cope with Northern Ghana's heat and dust, and a design the local contractor could repeat room by room.

## The design

**From the door, the bed first.** We moved the entry door hinge so the first thing a guest sees is the king bed against a cream panelled wall, with walnut nightstands, brass swing-arm sconces and a landscape painting above the headboard.

**One walnut sideboard, three jobs.** The opposite wall carries a single 3.4 m walnut sideboard with one unbroken top: a TV cabinet, an open writing desk with a mirror and lamp, and a minibar with open shelves. It replaces three separate pieces and keeps the floor clear.

![Walnut sideboard combining TV cabinet, writing desk and minibar](/projects/08-tamale-hotel-room-renovation/sideboard-desk-wall.webp)

**A lounge by the window.** Two sage-blue tub armchairs and a round walnut side table sit under the window, with sheer and camel blackout curtains. The split air conditioner is mounted high above them, and the linen drum pendant hides a ceiling fan, which the four-star standard requires.

![Window lounge with two sage-blue armchairs and a walnut side table](/projects/08-tamale-hotel-room-renovation/window-lounge.webp)

**A bathroom rebuilt for four stars.** The bathroom gains a bathtub under the window and a separate walk-in shower behind brass-framed tempered glass. The WC moves to the side wall, a 1200 mm walnut vanity takes the opposite wall, and the wet zone is sealed and set lower so the floor stays dry.

![Bathroom with bathtub, glass shower enclosure and walnut vanity](/projects/08-tamale-hotel-room-renovation/bathroom.webp)

## What we supply

- Bedroom floor: matt wood-look porcelain planks
- Walls: full-height cream panelling in fire-rated composite
- Ceiling: tray ceiling with crown moulding, warm LED cove and downlights
- Doors: walnut entry fire door with closer, viewer and key-card lock; walnut bathroom door
- Casegoods: walnut wardrobe, sideboard desk, nightstands, luggage rack
- Seating and bed: upholstered king bed and bench, tub armchairs, desk chair, in fire-retardant fabrics
- Lighting: brass sconces and lamps, linen drum pendant with concealed fan
- Bathroom: bathtub, thermostatic shower set, one-piece WC with bidet sprayer, vanity, glass enclosure, tiles
- Textiles: rug, sheer and blackout curtains

All items are specified for 230 V / 50 Hz with BS sockets, and joinery is sealed and treated against damp and termites.

## How it will be delivered

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Design approval | Layout, style and specification signed off by the owner |
| Samples | Fabrics, walnut finish, tiles and fittings approved in Foshan |
| Production | Furniture and finishes made and inspected in Foshan |
| Shipping | Consolidated in one container, shipped to Tema and cleared |
| Site works | About 32 days per room: demolition, services, waterproofing with a 48-hour flood test, ceiling, tiling, panels, joinery, fixtures, handover |

Rooms can run in parallel once all materials are on site.
