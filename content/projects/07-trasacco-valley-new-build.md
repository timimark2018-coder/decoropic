## The brief

A new villa in Trasacco Valley, with Decoropic working alongside the architect from the foundation stage so that the interior could be designed and sourced in parallel with construction. The centrepiece is a double-height living room opening onto the kitchen.

## What we supplied

- **Custom kitchen, dressing room and wardrobes** — Luxury tier
- **Interior doors** — Luxury tier
- **Sanitaryware and bathroom fittings** — Luxury tier
- **Tiles and flooring** — Luxury tier
- **Lighting** — Luxury tier

## How it was delivered (indicative, against a four-month build)

| Stage | What happened | Week |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Measurement | Measured from construction drawings, then verified on site as walls went up | 1–2 |
| 2. Design | Luxury scheme, lighting design, samples, itemised budget | 2–4 |
| 3. Sourcing | Orders placed early so production ran alongside construction | 4–9 |
| 4. QC & consolidation | Factory inspections, consolidation in Foshan | 9–10 |
| 5. Shipment & clearance | Sea freight to Tema, clearance, phased delivery to site | 10–13 |
| 6. Installation | Installation in sequence with the builder, snagging, handover | 13–17 |

## Challenges and how we handled them

- **Designing from drawings.** Typical for a new build, early measurements come from the architect rather than the site. Made-to-measure items were held until walls were plastered and verified, while tiles and sanitaryware were ordered from drawings.
- **Coordination with the builder.** Interior finishes depend on the builder's sequence. Delivery was phased so floors arrived before joinery and lighting, and the Accra team kept the installation plan aligned with the builder's programme.
- **Double-height space.** Lighting at height needs scaffolding and specialist fixing, so it was installed while the builder's access was still in place.

## Result

A Luxury-tier villa interior delivered in step with a four-month build and shipped in three containers, with a double-height living room, a custom kitchen and fully fitted bedrooms and bathrooms arriving on site when the builder needed them.
