High-ceiling private villas in Greater Accra often lose visual cohesion when tiles, aluminium glazing, dining furniture and feature lighting are bought from unrelated local shops. This 2026 design proposal coordinates the entire indoor-outdoor finish palette — from anti-slip pool-deck slabs to the double-height entrance foyer — into one consolidated Foshan shipment.

## Indoor-outdoor material continuity

- **Pool terrace & glazing:** R11 anti-slip porcelain outdoor tiles paired with corrosion-resistant powder-coated aluminium sliding doors that open the living space directly onto the pool deck.
- **Open-plan dining & living:** Seamless large-format marble-look porcelain flooring, bespoke dining table and chairs, concealed warm-white ceiling coves and a sculptural dining pendant.

![Open-plan villa dining and living area](/projects/10-accra-pool-villa-design/dining-living.webp)

- **Statement entrance foyer:** Full-height feature wall panelling, curated art plinth and layered accent lighting designed to photograph and welcome guests cleanly from the main door.
