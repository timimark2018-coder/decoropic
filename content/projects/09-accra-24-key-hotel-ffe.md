Repeating one coordinated guestroom and bathroom specification across 24 keys is the fastest way for a hotel owner in Accra to control per-room budget, guarantee visual consistency, and shorten installation time. This 2026 design proposal pairs warm timber-tone casegoods with moisture-resistant bathroom finishes engineered for Foshan factory consolidation.

## Repeatable room engineering

Instead of sourcing loose retail pieces locally, every wall-mounted and freestanding element was engineered as a single Foshan production run:

- **Sleep zone:** Upholstered commercial-grade bed frame and headboard panel with integrated bedside reading lights and 230V/50Hz Type G + USB sockets.
- **Work & storage wall:** Continuous timber-veneer TV console, writing desk and minibar recess designed to install cleanly across all 24 rooms with minimal on-site cutting.

![Continuous hotel TV unit, writing desk and wardrobe joinery](/projects/09-accra-24-key-hotel-ffe/guestroom-tv-desk.webp)

- **Bathroom package:** Batch-matched porcelain wall/floor tiles, moisture-resistant vanity with stone top, LED anti-fog mirror, tempered-glass shower enclosure and pressure-checked brassware — all packed together per room.
