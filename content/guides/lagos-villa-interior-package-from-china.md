# Lagos Villa Interior Package from China: What to Include and How It Ships

Buying interior finishing materials for a Lagos villa one category at a time — tiles from one dealer, a kitchen from another, doors from a third — usually costs more and takes longer than ordering the finishing package as a single consolidated shipment from Foshan. This guide covers what a villa package normally contains, how the three product tiers compare, what has to be measured before anything is made, and how a package reaches Apapa, Tin Can or Lekki and then your site in Lekki, Ikoyi, Victoria Island or the mainland.

## What goes into a villa finishing package

The package is everything above the structural shell that a finishing contractor would otherwise buy locally.

| Room / zone | Items typically in the package | Made to order? |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | Base and wall cabinets, worktop, sink, tap, hood | Yes — needs site dimensions |
| Bedrooms | Wardrobes, dressing units | Yes |
| Bathrooms | WC, basin, shower enclosure, mixers, accessories, mirror cabinet | Stock |
| Floors & walls | Porcelain or ceramic tiles, laminate or SPC flooring, skirting | Stock |
| Doors | Interior doors with frames and ironmongery, main entrance door | Semi-custom (size) |
| Windows | Aluminium or uPVC systems, glazing | Yes — needs opening sizes |
| Lighting | Downlights, pendants, wall lights, switches and sockets | Stock |
| Loose furniture | Sofas, dining, beds | Stock or semi-custom |

Structural and wet-trade materials (cement, blocks, rebar, sand, plaster) stay local. Paint is usually local too because of weight and VOC shipping rules.

## Three tiers, one container

| Tier | Who it suits | Kitchen example | Tile example |

| Functional | Rental units, staff quarters, budget-controlled builds | Melamine carcass and doors, laminate worktop | 600×600 ceramic |

You can mix tiers by room. A common Lagos pattern is Premium in the kitchen and master suite, Mid-Range elsewhere, Functional in the boys' quarters.

## Measure before you make

Custom kitchens, wardrobes and windows are made to the dimensions you send. For a Lagos site that means:

1. Floor plan and elevations (PDF or CAD).
2. Site measurements of each kitchen and wardrobe wall after plastering, with ceiling heights.
3. Window and door opening sizes after the structural frame is finished.
4. Photos of each room with a tape measure visible.

We return shop drawings for sign-off before production. If your contractor is unsure about measuring, a short video call with our team is usually enough to walk through it. The Accra office has done this for many Ghana sites; the method is identical for Lagos.

## Documents and ports for a villa shipment

A villa package is a normal commercial import into Nigeria. The sequence is covered in detail in our [import guide for Nigeria](/guides/import-building-materials-china-to-nigeria); the short version:

| Document | Handled by |
|---|---|
| SONCAP Product Certificate and SONCAP Certificate (regulated items such as tiles, sanitaryware, electricals) | SON-accredited agency; we provide factory access and test reports |
| Form M | Your Nigerian bank, using our pro forma invoice |
| PAAR | Your bank via the Nigeria Single Window |
| Commercial invoice (with Form M number), packing list, B/L, certificate of origin | Decoropic |
| SGD and duty payment | Your clearing agent in Lagos |

Published forwarder transit times from Shenzhen or Guangzhou to Apapa are about 27–39 days for a full container; add time for LCL. Lekki Deep Sea Port has operated commercially since 2023 and may suit sites on the Lekki–Epe axis; your forwarder confirms which port a given service calls.

Duty and VAT by HS code are for your clearing agent to quote.

## How much space does a villa take?

As a rule of thumb, a four- or five-bedroom villa's kitchen, wardrobes, doors and sanitaryware fit in a 20-foot container; add tiles for the whole house and a 40-foot is usually needed. Exact volume comes from the packing list once the product list is fixed. There is no minimum order, so a kitchen-and-wardrobes-only package can go LCL.

## Common mistakes on Lagos villa packages

- **Measuring before plastering.** Kitchen and wardrobe walls lose 20–40 mm after render; measure finished surfaces.
- **Forgetting the second-fix list.** Door stops, wardrobe interiors, tile trims, silicone and grout colour are easy to leave off and hard to match locally.
- **Opening the Form M on a draft pro forma.** Changes after Form M mean amendments at the bank; lock the product list first.
- **Ordering tiles without waste.** Allow 8–10 percent for cutting and breakage, more for diagonal or large-format layouts.
- **Mixing voltage standards.** Specify 220–240 V, Type G sockets for Nigeria on every lighting and electrical line.

## FAQ

**Can I buy only the kitchen and wardrobes?**
Yes. There is no minimum order. Small packages ship LCL; we will tell you when a full container becomes cheaper per item.

**Who fits the kitchen in Lagos?**
Your contractor or a local kitchen fitter. Cabinets arrive assembled or flat-packed, numbered to the drawing, with hardware bagged per unit. We support by video call.

**Are the tiles and sanitaryware SONCAP-compliant?**
We work with factories that hold the test reports SON-accredited agencies require. The Product Certificate and shipment certificate are issued by the agency, not by us.

**How do I pay?**
T/T or L/C. The pro forma invoice sets the schedule; typically a deposit on order and balance before shipment; the deposit is confirmed on your quote.
