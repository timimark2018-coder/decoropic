# How to Import Building Materials from China to Togo

Togo is a compact market with an outsized port. Lomé is the only deep-water container port in the country and one of the main transshipment hubs on the Gulf of Guinea, which makes it a straightforward destination to import building materials from China to Togo — and, for some buyers, a gateway to Burkina Faso, Niger and Mali. This guide covers the port, the documents (the BESC in particular), transit from South China, a category checklist and a timeline for villa, apartment and hotel projects in Lomé and beyond.

## Togo import documents

| Document | Full name / note | Who |
|---|---|---|
| BESC | Bordereau Électronique de Suivi des Cargaisons (electronic cargo tracking note, also called ECTN/CTN); mandatory for all cargo entering Togo or in transit; must be completed before the goods arrive at Lomé and before the customs declaration | Importer or forwarding agent, filed on the export side through the appointed agent |
| Certificate of origin | Required by the Togolese Revenue Office (OTR); the customs declaration must include a valid certificate of origin and its reference number | Decoropic (issued in China) |
| Commercial invoice and packing list | Itemised; French or bilingual recommended | Decoropic |
| Bill of lading | | Carrier via forwarder |
| Export declaration | Chinese export declaration | Decoropic |
| Permits for regulated goods | Not normally relevant to building materials; a 2020 decree requires pre-declaration with the Ministry of Trade for sensitive products | Importer |

Two cautions. The BESC deadline is before vessel arrival, so the forwarder must apply as soon as the B/L is issued; late submission is possible at destination but adds cost and delay. And the certificate of origin is no longer optional paperwork — OTR requires its reference number on the declaration.

Confirm with your clearing agent whether any conformity verification currently applies to tiles, sanitaryware or electrical items.

Duties follow the WAEMU common external tariff bands (0, 5, 10, 20 and 35 percent, with exceptions) plus VAT at 18 percent on most goods, per the US Commercial Guide. Which band applies to each product is by HS code and should be confirmed by your clearing agent.

For Lomé's coastal climate, ask for the coastal specification: moisture-resistant board for kitchens and wardrobes, stainless or coated hardware, powder-coated aluminium or uPVC windows with mosquito screens, IP65 exterior lighting and porcelain rather than ceramic floor tiles. These are standard options within each tier rather than upgrades.

## Common delays and how to avoid them

| Delay | Cause | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| BESC not validated on arrival | Filed after sailing | Forwarder applies from the draft B/L; we send BESC data the day the B/L is issued |
| Certificate of origin reference missing | Document not sent ahead | We send the scan with the shipping documents before arrival |
| Description or HS query | Vague line descriptions | HS code per line; bilingual packing list by room |
| Demurrage | Clearing started late | Agent pre-lodges the declaration; duty funds ready before arrival |
| Over-weight container | Tiles loaded by volume | Load tiles to road weight limit; split if needed |

## Payment and order size

T/T or L/C; no minimum order. For Togo's typically smaller projects LCL is common for a single villa; a 20-foot container suits a villa with full tiling, and 40-foot HC for apartments or hotels.

## FAQ

**Who files the BESC?**
Your forwarder or clearing agent, through the appointed BESC agent, using our B/L, invoice and packing list. It must be validated before the vessel arrives in Lomé.

**Can I use Lomé for a project in Burkina Faso or Niger?**
Yes. Lomé is a transit gateway; two BESCs are needed (Togo and destination country) and inland trucking is arranged by your forwarder.

**Do you issue documents in French?**
Yes, bilingual French/English invoices, packing lists and drawings on request.

**Can I see the products before ordering?**
At the Foshan warehouse, or at reference projects in Accra through our Ghana office.
