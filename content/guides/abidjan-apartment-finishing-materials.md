# Abidjan Apartment Finishing Materials from China (Matériaux de Finition)

Developers in Cocody, Marcory, Riviera and the newer zones of Abidjan who search for *matériaux de finition Abidjan* usually have a specific problem: a building of 10 to 100 apartments where every unit needs the same kitchen, the same bathroom set and the same floor, delivered on one schedule. Repeating units are exactly where consolidated supply from Foshan works best. This guide covers how an apartment finishing package is specified, the tiers, Côte d'Ivoire documents, container planning and a timeline for a multi-unit development.

## What an apartment finishing package includes

| Zone | Items | Repeat per unit? |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen | Base and wall cabinets, worktop, sink, mixer, hood | Yes — one design per unit type |
| Bathrooms | WC, basin or vanity, shower screen, mixers, accessories | Yes |
| Floors & walls | Porcelain tiles, SPC or laminate, skirting; bathroom wall tiles | Yes |
| Doors | Interior door sets, entrance door, ironmongery | Yes |
| Windows | Aluminium systems with glazing | Yes, by opening schedule |
| Lighting | Downlights, switches, sockets | Yes |

The repeat structure matters: 40 identical kitchens are cheaper per unit and faster to produce than 40 different ones, and installation crews learn the layout after the first floor.

## Measuring and drawings for repeat units

Because the units repeat, measurement is simpler than for a villa but errors multiply. We need:

1. Architectural plans for each unit type (CAD preferred).
2. Site measurements of one built unit per type after plastering — kitchen walls, bathroom dimensions, door and window openings.
3. A door and window schedule by unit type.
4. Tile areas by unit type and common area.

We return shop drawings per unit type (French or English) for sign-off. One kitchen and one bathroom set can be produced and shipped ahead as a sample unit if the schedule allows.

## Documents and port

The shipment is a normal import into Côte d'Ivoire. Full detail is in our [Côte d'Ivoire import guide](/guides/import-building-materials-china-to-cote-divoire); in summary:

| Document | Handled by |
|---|---|
| FDI (Fiche de Déclaration à l'Importation) via GUCE | Importer or clearing agent, from our pro forma invoice |
| VoC Certificate of Conformity (Bureau Veritas programme; building materials and electrical products are covered categories) | Inspection arranged at Foshan; we provide factory access and documents |
| BSC (Bordereau de Suivi des Cargaisons) from OIC | Clearing agent, from our B/L and invoice |
| Commercial invoice, packing list, B/L, certificate of origin (bilingual on request) | Decoropic |
| Customs declaration, duty and VAT | Your transitaire; rates by HS code not quoted by Decoropic |

Forwarder-published FCL transit from Shenzhen to Abidjan is about 45 days.

## Installation sequencing on site

Repeat units reward a fixed sequence per floor. The order that works on most Abidjan sites:

1. **Windows** first, so the units are weather-tight before finishes go in.
2. **Floor and wall tiles** in bathrooms and kitchens, then living areas.
3. **Door frames** and then door leaves after painting.
4. **Bathroom sanitaryware** on finished tiles; shower screens last.
5. **Kitchens** once floors and wall tiles are complete and electrical points are live.
6. **Wardrobes** after painting and flooring.
7. **Lighting** final fix.

Our packing list follows this order, floor by floor, so the containers can be unloaded into a staging area and drawn down by trade. Each kitchen and wardrobe carton is marked with the unit number and the drawing reference. A video guide per unit type covers cabinet hanging, worktop seams, shower screen fixing and door hardware; your site team can reach us on WhatsApp during working hours in West Africa.
