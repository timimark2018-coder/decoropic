# Hotel Furniture and FF&E Supply from China to Nigeria

Developers looking for hotel furniture suppliers in Nigeria usually end up comparing three routes: local joinery, a European FF&E agent, or direct supply from China. For a 30- to 200-key property in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt or a state capital, consolidated supply from Foshan — the centre of China's furniture and building-materials manufacturing — is often the practical middle ground on cost, finish consistency and lead time. This guide explains what a hotel FF&E package from China contains, how it is specified, what Nigerian import documents apply, and how to plan containers and timelines.

## What a hotel FF&E package covers

FF&E (furniture, fixtures and equipment) for a hotel is normally grouped by area. Decoropic covers the furniture and finishes side; kitchen equipment, laundry and IT are outside our scope.

| Area | Typical items | Decoropic category |
|---|---|---|
| Guest rooms | Bed base and headboard, nightstands, desk, chair, wardrobe, luggage bench, minibar cabinet, mirror | Furniture (hotel FF&E), custom wardrobes |
| Guest bathrooms | WC, basin and vanity, shower enclosure, mixers, accessories | Sanitaryware & hardware |
| Room finishes | Floor tiles or SPC, wall tiles, doors with hotel locks | Tiles & flooring, doors |
| Corridors & lobby | Lobby seating, reception desk, decorative lighting, floor tiles | Furniture, lighting, tiles |
| F&B | Dining tables and chairs, bar stools, banquettes | Furniture |
| Lighting throughout | Downlights, bedside lamps, decorative pendants | Lighting |

Mattresses, curtains and soft furnishings are available on request, or can be sourced locally.

## Specification: how a room package is built

A hotel order starts from a room type schedule, not a product catalogue.

1. **Room type list** — number of keys per type (standard, deluxe, suite) and the room dimensions.
2. **Mood or reference images** — or a designer's FF&E schedule if one exists.
3. **Tier selection** — Functional for budget or business hotels, Mid-Range for 3–4 star, Premium for upscale.
4. **Model room** — we produce one room set first for approval; production of all keys follows sign-off.
5. **Shop drawings** — every casegood piece dimensioned, with finish and hardware codes.

The model-room step is the point where most issues get caught: headboard height against the socket positions, wardrobe depth against the room width, desk cable management. It is worth the extra two to three weeks.

## Durability requirements for hotel use

Hotel furniture is specified differently from residential. Ask for, and we will provide: moisture-resistant board for wet-climate sites, high-pressure laminate or lacquer on high-wear surfaces, commercial-grade drawer runners and hinges, contract-grade upholstery fabrics, and sanitaryware with replaceable cartridges and locally available spare parts. Finish samples are sent before the model room.

## Nigeria import documents for hotel FF&E

A hotel shipment is larger but follows the same sequence as any commercial import.

| Document | Who | Note |
|---|---|---|
| SONCAP Product Certificate / Certificate | SON-accredited agency | Applies to regulated lines (typically tiles, sanitaryware, lighting, electrical items); furniture scope to be confirmed by the agency |
| Form M | Importer's bank | One Form M per pro forma invoice; large orders may be split into several shipments under one or more Form Ms, confirmed on your quote |
| PAAR | Bank via Nigeria Single Window | Per shipment |
| Commercial invoice, packing list, B/L, certificate of origin | Decoropic | Packing list by room type and floor speeds installation |
| SGD, duty payment | Clearing agent | Duty by HS code; not quoted by Decoropic |

Transit from Shenzhen or Guangzhou to Apapa is published by forwarders at roughly 27–39 days for a full container. Lekki Deep Sea Port, in commercial operation since 2023, is an alternative gateway for some services.

## Container planning for a hotel

| Hotel size | Indicative container count (furniture + bathrooms + lighting, excluding tiles) |
|---|---|
| 30–50 keys | 2–4 × 40-foot HC |
| 80–120 keys | 5–9 × 40-foot HC |
| 150–200 keys | 10+ × 40-foot HC |

These are planning figures only; the packing list gives the real number, and your exact count is confirmed on your quote. Tiles for public areas and rooms add weight-limited containers on top. Phased shipping — floors 1–3 first, then 4–6 — lets installation start while production continues.

## Timeline

| Phase | Weeks |

Five to seven months from brief to first containers on site is typical for a mid-size hotel. Phased shipping shortens the time to first installed floor.

## Spares and after-sales

A hotel order should include an attic stock of consumable and high-wear items so housekeeping and maintenance are not waiting on a container: mixer cartridges, drawer runners, hinges, toilet seats, lamp drivers, a few spare headboards and chairs, and 3–5 percent extra tiles per type. We list spares as separate lines on the packing list so they are stored, not installed. Replacement parts for later years are ordered the same way, by WhatsApp with the original order reference, and shipped LCL or by air.

## FAQ

**Do you supply a model room before full production?**
Yes. One complete room set is produced, photographed and, if you wish, shipped ahead for approval before all keys go into production.

**Can furniture be shipped assembled?**
Casegoods are shipped assembled where the piece is rigid (nightstands, desks) and knock-down where it saves significant volume (wardrobes, headboards). The packing list states which.

**Who installs?**
Your fit-out contractor. We provide installation drawings per room type and video guides; our team is available by WhatsApp during installation.

**What payment terms apply to a hotel order?**
T/T or L/C. Large orders are normally structured with a deposit, a payment on model-room approval and the balance before each shipment; the schedule is confirmed on your quote.
