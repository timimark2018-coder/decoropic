# How to Import Building Materials from China to Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire is one of the more orderly markets in West Africa to import building materials from China into: a single electronic window (GUCE), a well-run container port in Abidjan and a conformity programme that is clear about what it covers. The sequence still has to be followed correctly. This guide covers the port, the three documents that matter most (FDI, BSC and the VoC Certificate of Conformity), transit times from South China, a category checklist and a timeline for a villa, apartment or hotel project in Abidjan or upcountry.

## Port of Abidjan and transit from South China

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Main port | Port Autonome d'Abidjan (Abidjan), Gulf of Guinea |
| Secondary port | San Pédro (south-west; used for some cargo) |
| Origin ports we use | Guangzhou (Nansha) or Shenzhen (Yantian/Shekou), from the Foshan warehouse |
| Published FCL transit, Shenzhen → Abidjan | About 45 days (forwarder-published figure) |
| Published LCL transit | About 52–55 days |
| Air freight (samples only) | 3–7 days to Abidjan (ABJ) |

Transit depends on the carrier and whether the service is direct or relays via a hub, so treat the figures as planning numbers and ask your forwarder for the current schedule. Decoropic books with your forwarder or recommends one with Abidjan experience; clearing is done by your transitaire (clearing agent) in Abidjan.

## The three Côte d'Ivoire documents you must get right

| Document | Full name | Issued / managed by | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| FDI | Fiche de Déclaration à l'Importation | Prepared through the GUCE single window by the importer or agent | Before shipment; records importer, exporter, HS codes, CIF value, transport details |
| BSC | Bordereau de Suivi des Cargaisons (also called ECTN) | Office Ivoirien des Chargeurs (OIC), electronically | Validated before vessel arrival; references the FDI |
| VoC Certificate of Conformity | Certificate of Conformity under the Verification of Conformity (VoC) programme | Ministry of Commerce programme implemented by Bureau Veritas; mandatory since July 2018 | Goods assessed before export; required for customs clearance |

The VoC programme covers thirteen product categories including building materials and electrical and electronic products. In practice that means tiles, sanitaryware, doors and windows, lighting and electrical accessories in a Decoropic order are likely within scope; exact inclusion per HS code is confirmed by Bureau Veritas at the time of application.

Standard commercial documents from our side: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin. Documents in French or bilingual French/English are preferable since the customs administration works in French; we issue bilingual invoices and packing lists on request.

Duty and VAT are applied by HS code under the regional common external tariff. We do not quote rates; your transitaire does.

## Timeline

| Phase | Weeks | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Brief, drawings, pro forma | 1–2 | Pro forma invoice feeds the FDI |
| FDI and VoC application | 2–3 | Runs in parallel with production |
| Production and consolidation at Foshan | 4–8 | Custom joinery is the long pole |
| VoC inspection and Certificate of Conformity | 1–2 | Inspection at Foshan warehouse before loading |
| BSC validation | Before vessel arrival | Through OIC via your agent |
| Sea freight | 6–8 | Shenzhen/Guangzhou → Abidjan |
| Clearing and delivery | 1–2 | Transitaire in Abidjan |

Allow four to six months from brief to materials on site.

## Common delays and how to avoid them

| Delay | Cause | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| VoC certificate not ready at loading | Application started after production finished | Apply when the pro forma is final, inspect during consolidation |
| BSC not validated on arrival | Agent waited for original B/L | Apply from the telex or draft B/L the day it is issued |
| FDI value mismatch | Final invoice differs from pro forma | Freeze the product list before FDI; amend only if essential |
| Customs query on description | Furniture described as "board" or tiles without m² | Use clear descriptions and HS codes per line; bilingual packing list |
| Container held for weight | Tiles over road weight limit | Load tiles to weight, not volume; split across containers |

## Payment and order size

T/T or L/C; no minimum order. A single apartment's kitchen and bathrooms can ship LCL. Mixed categories from multiple Foshan-area factories are consolidated into one container with one set of documents, which also simplifies the single FDI and BSC.

## FAQ

**Who applies for the BSC?**
The BSC is obtained through the Office Ivoirien des Chargeurs, normally by your clearing agent or a BSC service provider, using the bill of lading and invoice we issue. We provide the data; we do not apply on your behalf.

**Is the VoC inspection done in China?**
Yes. Goods are assessed before export; Bureau Veritas inspects at the Foshan warehouse or factory and issues the Certificate of Conformity used at clearance.

**Can documents be in French?**
Yes. We issue bilingual French/English invoices, packing lists and shop drawings on request.

**Do you deliver to Yamoussoukro, Bouaké or San Pédro?**
Delivery is to Port of Abidjan (or San Pédro if your forwarder routes there). Inland trucking is arranged by your transitaire.
