# Hotel FF&E Checklist: A Room-by-Room List of Items

A complete list of FF&E items for hotels saves money twice: once when you budget, because nothing is forgotten, and again on site, because nothing arrives late. This checklist works room by room, separates FF&E (furniture, fixtures and equipment) from OS&E (operating supplies and equipment), and notes the specifications you need to decide for each item. It is written for owners and developers in Ghana, with notes on the guest room items set out in the national accommodation standards.

## FF&E versus OS&E

Industry usage draws a broad line between the two:

| | FF&E | OS&E |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Long-life furniture, fixtures and equipment | Smaller items used daily and replaced or restocked regularly |
| Examples | Beds, wardrobes, desks, vanities, lighting, curtains, reception desk | Linen, towels, toiletries, hangers, glassware, cleaning carts |
| Budget treatment | Capital cost, depreciated | Pre-opening supply, then operating cost |
| Replacement cycle | Years | Weeks to months |

Some items, such as hairdryers, kettles, safes, lamps and mini-fridges, sit on either list depending on the operator's accounting rules. Decide early which list each belongs to, so it is neither double-counted nor missed. This guide focuses on FF&E, with OS&E listed so you can see where the boundary falls. For the buying process itself, see our [hotel FF&E procurement guide](/guides/hotel-ffe-procurement-ghana).

| Luggage rack | FF&E | Fixed or folding, finish to match casegoods |
| Dressing table and mirror | FF&E | Combined with desk or separate |

| Curtains, sheers and blackout | FF&E | Track type, blackout level, fabric treatment |
| Lighting: ceiling, bedside, desk, floor | FF&E | Colour temperature, switching, dimming |

| Minibar unit | FF&E or OS&E | Absorption (quiet) or compressor, cabinet recess |

| Flooring and wall finishes | FF&E (fit-out) | Tile, vinyl, carpet, wall panel |
| Bed linen, pillows, duvets | OS&E | Thread count, par levels |
| Hangers, kettle, tray, glasses | OS&E | Quantity per room |

**Ghana four-star note.** Ghana's accommodation regulations (L.I. 2239, 2016) set out the guest room provisions for each star grade. For four-star rooms these include items such as a writing desk and chair, an armchair per occupant, a luggage rack, a dressing table with mirror, a wardrobe, a priced minibar, remote-controlled air conditioning, a television and a telephone. Lower grades have fewer requirements. Check the current regulations and your grading inspector's guidance before fixing your room list.

## Specs that apply to every item

Whatever the room, the same decisions come up. Settling them once, in a finish schedule, keeps items consistent and quotes comparable.

| Decision | Options to fix | Why it matters in Ghana |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Solid timber, veneered board, laminate, metal | Humidity and termites affect timber and board |
| Finish | Colour code, sheen, edge-banding | Matching across suppliers and reorders |
| Fabric | Weight, rub-test rating, colour, treatment | Heat, humidity and heavy use |
| Dimensions | Overall size, door and lift clearances | Pieces must fit through doors and stairs |
| Fixings | Wall fixing, levelling feet | Site walls vary |
| Spares | Extra hinges, handles, fabric | Local matching parts may be hard to find |
| Packing | Flat-pack or assembled, carton labelling | Container volume and site assembly time |

## FAQ

**What is the difference between FF&E and OS&E?**
FF&E covers long-life furniture, fixtures and equipment. OS&E covers smaller operating items, such as linen and glassware, that are replaced regularly.

**What does a four-star guest room need in Ghana?**
The national accommodation regulations list items such as a writing desk and chair, armchairs, a luggage rack, a wardrobe, a priced minibar, remote-controlled air conditioning, TV and telephone. Confirm the current list with the grading authority.

**When should FF&E be ordered?**
Once the layout, finish schedule and room counts are fixed, allowing for production, inspection, shipping and clearance before your installation date.

**Should I buy FF&E and OS&E from the same supplier?**
It can simplify logistics, but the two are specified differently. Many owners source FF&E as a project package and OS&E closer to opening.

**Can one container carry furniture, sanitaryware and lighting together?**
