# Hotel Fit-Out in Abidjan: FF&E and Finishes Supplied from China

A hotel fit-out in Abidjan — whether a 40-key business hotel in Plateau, a resort property on the lagoon or a mid-size hotel in Yamoussoukro or San Pédro — needs furniture, bathrooms, lighting, doors and floors delivered in a coordinated sequence. Supplying these from Foshan as one specified package, shipped in phases to Port of Abidjan, is a route many West African developers use. This guide covers scope, specification, Côte d'Ivoire documents, container planning and a timeline.

## Scope of a hotel fit-out package

| Area | Items | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Guest rooms | Bed, headboard, nightstands, desk, chair, wardrobe, luggage rack, minibar unit, mirror | Hotel FF&E, custom wardrobes |
| Guest bathrooms | WC, vanity and basin, shower enclosure, mixers, accessories | Sanitaryware & hardware |
| Room finishes | Floor and wall tiles or SPC, door sets with hotel locks | Tiles & flooring, doors |
| Lobby and corridors | Reception desk, lounge seating, feature lighting, floor tiles | Furniture, lighting, tiles |
| Restaurant and bar | Tables, chairs, banquettes, bar stools, pendant lighting | Furniture, lighting |
| Meeting rooms | Tables, chairs, acoustic doors | Furniture, doors |

Kitchen equipment, laundry, HVAC, IT and operating supplies are outside Decoropic's scope. Mattresses and curtains are available on request, or can be sourced locally.

## Specification and the model room

1. **Room type schedule** — keys per type with dimensions.
2. **Design references** — designer's FF&E schedule or mood boards.
3. **Tier** — Functional (budget/business), Mid-Range (3–4 star), Premium (upscale).
4. **Model room** — one complete room produced for approval before full production; can be shipped ahead or approved by video and photos.
5. **Shop drawings** — per piece, bilingual French/English.

Hotel-grade specifications are standard: moisture-resistant board for the humid coastal climate, HPL or lacquer on high-wear surfaces, commercial drawer runners and hinges, contract-grade upholstery, sanitaryware with replaceable cartridges.

Electrical items are specified for 220–240 V with Type C/E outlets; hotel locks, safes and minibars are selected to the operator's brand standard if there is one, or from commercial ranges if the hotel is independent. If an international operator is involved, their FF&E brand standard is the starting document and we specify to it; for independent hotels we propose a schedule from the room type list and the tier chosen.

## Côte d'Ivoire documents for a hotel shipment

| Document | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FDI (Fiche de Déclaration à l'Importation) | Importer or agent via GUCE | One per shipment; from our pro forma invoice |
| VoC Certificate of Conformity | Bureau Veritas programme (Ministry of Commerce), mandatory since 2018; building materials and electrical products are covered categories | Inspection at Foshan before loading; furniture scope confirmed per HS code |
| BSC (Bordereau de Suivi des Cargaisons) | Office Ivoirien des Chargeurs | Validated before vessel arrival |
| Commercial invoice, packing list by area and floor, B/L, certificate of origin | Decoropic | Bilingual on request |
| Customs declaration, duty, VAT | Transitaire | Rates by HS code; not quoted by Decoropic |

Forwarder-published FCL transit Shenzhen → Abidjan is about 45 days; confirm with your forwarder for the specific service.

## Public areas and custom pieces

Guest rooms are the volume; public areas are where a hotel is judged. Reception desks, back-bar units, banquette seating, feature lighting and lobby flooring are made to drawings rather than chosen from a range. For these we work from the designer's drawings or produce our own, send finish samples (stone, veneer, laminate, fabric, metal), and build the piece after sign-off. Large or fragile items such as a stone-clad reception desk are crated separately and flagged on the packing list for first unloading.

## Spares, maintenance and reorders

Include attic stock in the first order: mixer cartridges, hinges and runners, toilet seats, LED drivers, spare chairs and headboards, and 3–5 percent extra tiles per type. Spares are listed and packed separately so they reach the maintenance store, not the rooms. Reorders in later years quote the original order reference; small quantities ship LCL to Abidjan or by air for urgent parts.

## FAQ

**Can the model room be inspected in person?**
Yes, at the Foshan warehouse, or by video walkthrough and photo set. Many clients combine the Foshan visit with factory visits for tiles and sanitaryware.

**Can public-area furniture be custom?**
Yes. Reception desks, banquettes and feature pieces are made to drawings; loose seating is selected from hotel-grade ranges.

**Does the VoC certificate cover furniture?**
The VoC programme's covered categories include building materials and electrical products. Whether specific furniture HS codes are in scope is confirmed by Bureau Veritas at application.

**What payment terms apply?**
T/T or L/C, normally a deposit, a payment on model-room approval and the balance before each shipment; the schedule is confirmed on your quote.
