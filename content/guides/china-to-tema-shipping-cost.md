# China to Tema Container Cost: Every Component Explained

Ask three forwarders for the China to Tema container cost and you may get three very different numbers, often because each quote covers a different part of the journey. This guide does not list prices: freight rates change week to week, and duties depend on what is inside the box. Instead, it breaks the total into its parts, explains what drives each one, and gives you a checklist for comparing quotes so you can see what is included and what is left out.

## Why one "container price" is never the whole cost

A shipment from Shenzhen or Nansha to a site in Ghana passes through several hands: the factory, the origin trucker, the origin terminal, the shipping line, the Tema terminal, customs, the clearing agent and the haulier. Each charges for its part. A quote that says "40HQ to Tema" may cover only the ocean leg, leaving destination charges, duties and delivery to arrive as separate invoices.

The Incoterm on your purchase contract decides who pays which part. Under FOB you pay from the moment goods are on board in China; under CIF the seller pays freight and minimum insurance to Tema; under DDP the seller covers everything to a named place in Ghana. Always read a quote alongside its Incoterm.

## The cost components

| Component | What it pays for | Paid to | Usually under FOB | Usually under CIF |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Origin trucking and loading | Moving goods from factory or warehouse to the port; stuffing the container | Trucker, warehouse | Seller | Seller |
| Origin THC | Terminal handling at Shenzhen / Nansha | Terminal via line or forwarder | Seller | Seller |
| Export documentation and customs | Export declaration, B/L issue, certificates | Forwarder, customs broker | Seller | Seller |
| Ocean freight | Base rate for the container from origin port to Tema | Shipping line | Buyer | Seller |
| BAF and other surcharges | Fuel adjustment, peak season, emergency or congestion surcharges | Shipping line | Buyer | Seller (if quoted all-in) |
| Cargo insurance | Marine cover for loss or damage | Insurer | Buyer | Seller (minimum cover) |
| Destination THC and line local charges | Terminal handling at Tema, delivery order, container-related fees | Line, terminal | Buyer | Buyer |
| Import duties and taxes | Duty, VAT and levies assessed on the declared value | Ghana Revenue Authority via ICUMS | Buyer | Buyer |
| Clearing agent fees | Preparing the declaration, managing examination and release | Licensed clearing agent | Buyer | Buyer |
| Haulage to site | Trucking from Tema to Accra, Kumasi, Tamale or elsewhere | Haulier | Buyer | Buyer |
| Container return and storage | Returning the empty box; port storage if delayed | Line, terminal | Buyer | Buyer |

The clearance stage itself is covered in our [Tema port clearing guide](/guides/tema-port-clearing-building-materials); here we look only at what drives its cost.

## What drives each component

| Component | Main cost drivers |
|---|---|
| Ocean freight | Container size (20GP, 40GP, 40HQ), carrier, season, global capacity, booking lead time, direct versus transhipment routing |
| Surcharges | Fuel prices, peak periods such as before Chinese New Year, disruption on major routes, congestion at either end |
| Origin charges | Distance from factory to port, number of pick-ups, whether loading is done at a warehouse or factory |
| Insurance | Insured value, cover level (Clauses A versus C), type of goods, packing quality |
| Duties and taxes | HS classification of each item, customs value, origin of goods, any exemptions that apply to the project |
| Clearing fees | Number of item lines on the declaration, whether physical examination is ordered, accuracy of documents |
| Destination port charges | Time the container stays at the terminal; free days granted by the line |
| Haulage | Distance from Tema, road conditions, access to site, whether a crane or forklift is needed |
| Demurrage and detention | Delays in clearance, payment or unloading beyond the free time |

Two drivers deserve special attention.

**HS classification and valuation.** Duties are calculated on the customs value and the tariff line for each product. A container carrying tiles, sanitaryware, furniture and lighting will have several tariff lines. Mis-described goods are a common cause of delay and extra cost. Exact rates should be confirmed with your clearing agent for each shipment.

**Time at Tema.** Shipping lines allow a set number of free days for the container at the port (demurrage) and away from the port before it is returned empty (detention). After that, daily charges apply. Most avoidable destination cost comes from documents that are not ready when the ship arrives.

## Container size and cost per cubic metre

The ocean freight for a 40ft container is generally not double the 20ft rate, so the cost per cubic metre often falls as the container gets bigger. That is why consolidating several categories into one larger container usually reduces the cost per item.

| Container | Nominal internal capacity | Typical use for building and furnishing goods |
|---|---|---|
| 20GP | about 33 m³ | Heavy, dense goods such as tiles or stone, where weight fills the box before volume |
| 40GP | about 67.5 m³ | Mixed loads with moderate volume |
| 40HQ | about 76.4 m³ | Bulky goods such as furniture, doors, cabinets and mattresses |

Heavy goods may reach the weight limit before filling the space; light, bulky goods fill the space first. A good load plan balances both. See our [container consolidation guide](/guides/container-consolidation-house-finishing-ghana) for how a whole house is planned this way.

## How to compare quotes

When two quotes are far apart, it is usually because they cover different things. Line them up against the same list:

1. **Same Incoterm and same named place.** "CIF Tema" and "DDP Accra site" are not comparable.
2. **Same container size and count.**
3. **Surcharges itemised or "all-in"?** Ask whether BAF and seasonal surcharges are included and for how long the rate is valid.
4. **Destination charges stated or excluded?** Many low quotes exclude Tema terminal and line local charges.
5. **Duties: estimated, included or excluded?** If "included", ask for the HS codes used.
6. **Insurance level.** Clauses C and Clauses A are very different products.
7. **Free days at Tema.** More free time can be worth more than a small freight saving.
8. **Delivery to site.** Is haulage included, and to which address?
9. **Validity.** Freight quotes often expire within days or weeks.

## Hidden-cost checklist

Before you approve a shipment, tick each line:

| Check | Why it can cost you |
|---|---|
| Packing list matches the commercial invoice item by item | Mismatches trigger queries and examination |
| HS codes agreed with the clearing agent before shipping | Reclassification changes duty after arrival |
| Original or telex-release B/L ready before arrival | Without it, the container cannot be released |
| Payment of duties planned before the ship berths | Delays eat into free days |
| Free days confirmed in writing by the line or forwarder | Demurrage and detention accrue daily |
| Site ready to receive and unload | Truck waiting time and re-delivery |
| Insurance certificate issued before departure | Cover may not apply retroactively |
| Fragile items crated and photographed | Damage claims need evidence |
| Spare hardware and fittings packed | Local replacements may not match |

## FAQ

**How much does a container from China to Tema cost?**
It depends on container size, season, surcharges, the goods inside and how far the site is from Tema. We give an itemised quote per shipment rather than a single headline figure.

**Why do freight rates change so often?**
Ocean freight responds to fuel prices, capacity and demand. Surcharges can be added or removed at short notice, which is why quotes carry a validity date.

**Are import duties included in a CIF price?**
No. Under CIF, import duties and destination charges at Tema are paid by the buyer.

**Is a 40HQ always better value than a 20GP?**
For bulky goods, the cost per cubic metre is usually lower in a 40HQ. For heavy goods such as tiles, a 20GP may reach its weight limit first, so the larger box adds little.

**How do I avoid demurrage at Tema?**
Have documents, HS codes and duty payment ready before the vessel arrives, and confirm your free days in writing.
