# Hotel Furniture Suppliers in Ghana: How to Procure an FF&E Package for 20 to 100 Rooms

Owners searching for hotel furniture suppliers Ghana are usually at the point where the building is nearly done and the rooms are empty. The decision is bigger than furniture: a hotel FF&E package covers casegoods, seating, beds, lighting, bathroom fittings, doors, window treatments and the public areas, and the quality of procurement determines whether rooms open on time and survive their first three years of use. This guide explains how to scope an FF&E package for a 20 to 100 room hotel in Ghana, why a mock-up room pays for itself, how to run quality control, and how to phase delivery.

## What an FF&E package includes

FF&E stands for furniture, fixtures and equipment: the items that are not part of the building fabric and that a hotel operator needs to run rooms. For a Ghana property sourced from China, the package typically breaks down as follows.

| Area | Items | Notes for Ghana |
|---|---|---|
| Guestroom casegoods | Headboard, bed base, bedside tables, desk, luggage bench, wardrobe, minibar cabinet, TV panel | Moisture-resistant board, sealed edges; wardrobe to ceiling where possible |
| Guestroom seating | Desk chair, armchair, sofa in suites | Upholstery with high rub count; removable covers for cleaning |
| Mattresses and bedding | Mattress, protector, pillows | Fire-retardant specification; confirm bed base dimensions |
| Guestroom lighting | Bedside, desk, reading, ceiling, bathroom mirror light | 230 V, 50 Hz; LED drivers rated for high ambient temperature |
| Bathroom | WC, basin, shower set, mirror, accessories, glass screen | Wall-hung or floor-standing by tier; spares for consumables |
| Doors and ironmongery | Entry door with lock system, bathroom door, wardrobe doors | Electronic lock compatible with the operator's system |
| Window treatments | Blackout and sheer curtains, tracks | Flame-retardant fabric |
| Public areas | Reception desk, lobby seating, restaurant tables and chairs, bar, meeting-room furniture | Heavier-duty construction than guestrooms |
| Back of house | Staff lockers, housekeeping trolleys, luggage trolleys | Often sourced separately |

Operating supplies and equipment (OS&E), such as linen, crockery and small appliances, is a separate package with a different supplier base and should be budgeted separately.

## Scoping: from room count to a bill of quantities

Start with the room matrix: how many standard rooms, twins, suites and accessible rooms, and the exact internal dimensions of each type after plastering. Every FF&E quantity derives from that matrix. Then set a tier for the property: Functional for a budget business hotel, Mid-Range for a three to four star property, Premium for a boutique or upper-upscale product. Decoropic designs to these three tiers and can hold the room layout constant while moving the specification.

Measure a sample of rooms of each type on site. Hotel rooms on the same floor plan are rarely identical in built dimensions, and wardrobes, desks and headboards that fit the drawing may not fit the room.

## The mock-up room

A mock-up room is one complete guestroom furnished and fitted to the proposed specification before the full order is placed. For a 20 to 100 room hotel, it is the most useful money spent in procurement.

What the mock-up tests:

- Fit of every item in the real room, including clearance for doors and drawers
- Finish and colour under the room's actual lighting
- Durability concerns: edge banding, drawer runners, upholstery, lamp switches
- Housekeeping practicality: can the bed be made, can the floor be mopped under the furniture
- Electrical positions: sockets at the desk and bedside, USB, switch locations
- Bathroom fittings: water pressure at the shower, drain speed, mirror light colour

Fix every change in writing, update the specification, and only then release production. A mock-up room can be built in the hotel itself or, for speed, as a sample set at the factory with photos and video; the on-site mock-up is better because it exposes building tolerances.

## Quality control for a multi-room order

A hotel order is dozens of identical items. The failure mode is a defect repeated across every room.

| QC stage | What is checked | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-production | Materials and hardware samples approved against the mock-up | Supplier QC with client sign-off |
| In production | First-off inspection of each item type before the batch runs | Supplier QC |
| Pre-shipment | Random sampling against the approved specification; dimensions, finish, hardware, packaging | Supplier QC, optionally a third-party inspector |
| At loading | Count against packing list; photo record of every pallet | Supplier warehouse |
| On delivery | Count and damage check against packing list, room by room | Client's representative with supplier technician |

Keep the approved mock-up room sealed as the reference until the last room is installed.

## Phased delivery and logistics

A 60-room hotel's FF&E does not fit in one container, and even if it did, the site could not absorb it at once. Phase delivery by floor or by wing, in the order the building is being handed over, and consolidate each phase as a mixed container carrying everything that floor needs: casegoods, lighting, bathroom fittings, doors and curtains.

Points to plan:

- **Sea transit** from southern China to Tema is roughly 30 days port to port; forwarders quote full-container times of 32 to 42 days.
- **Clearance** at Tema runs through ICUMS and requires the bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, Import Declaration Form and importer registration; electrical goods may fall under Ghana Standards Authority conformity requirements, so confirm coverage before shipment.
- **Storage on site**: a dry, lockable area large enough for one phase at a time.
- **Installation crew**: the supplier's technician supervising local carpenters and fitters; set a rooms-per-day target and hold it.
- **Spares**: ship attic stock of hinges, runners, lamps, shower cartridges and upholstery fabric with the final phase.

## Payment structure for hotel orders

Hotel FF&E is normally paid by milestone: a deposit on order, a payment on approval of the mock-up or pre-shipment inspection, and the balance against shipping documents, with installation supervision invoiced separately. T/T and L/C are both workable; an L/C suits owners whose lenders want documentary control over the goods portion. Percentages are agreed at quotation.

## FAQ

**Can a Ghana hotel source furniture locally instead?**
Some items, such as simple restaurant seating or back-of-house shelving, can be made locally. Matched guestroom casegoods in volume, with consistent finish and hardware, are hard to produce locally at hotel durability, which is why most 20 to 100 room properties import the guestroom package.

**How many containers does a 50-room hotel need?**
It depends on the specification and how much is flat-packed. Casegoods, mattresses and seating are bulky; a supplier will give a container plan from the bill of quantities. Expect multiple 40-foot containers phased by floor.

**Do mattresses and curtains need any certificate in Ghana?**
Fire-retardant specifications are a matter for the operator's brand standard and the insurer. Check with the Ghana Standards Authority and the fire service on current requirements for the property class; do not assume the exporting country's certificate is sufficient.

**What is the lead time for a hotel FF&E order?**
Allow design and mock-up, production of — confirmed with your quote depending on scope, consolidation, about 30 days at sea, clearance and phased installation. The mock-up stage is the part owners most often underestimate.
