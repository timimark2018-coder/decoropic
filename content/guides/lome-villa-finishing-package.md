# Villa Finishing in Lomé: A Complete Interior Package from China

Villa finishing in Lomé — in Agoè, Baguida, Avépozo or the newer residential zones along the coast road — typically runs into the same issue: the structure is up, but sourcing the kitchen, bathrooms, tiles, doors and lighting locally means many suppliers, uneven quality and long waits. A single consolidated finishing package from Foshan, shipped to Port of Lomé, replaces that with one order, one container and one schedule. This guide explains what goes in the package, the tiers, measurement, documents, container size and timeline.

## Measuring before production

1. Floor plans and elevations (PDF or CAD).
2. Kitchen and wardrobe wall measurements after plastering, with ceiling heights.
3. Door and window opening sizes after the frame is complete.
4. Photos of each room with a tape measure in view.

We return shop drawings (French or English) for sign-off before anything is made. A short video call with your mason or carpenter usually resolves any measuring questions.

## Coastal climate: what to specify for Lomé

Lomé's coastal humidity and salt air affect material choice more than in inland cities.

| Item | What to specify | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen and wardrobe carcass | Moisture-resistant board, edge-banded all round | Swelling at cut edges in humid rooms |
| Hardware | Stainless or coated hinges, runners and handles | Corrosion near the coast |
| Windows | Powder-coated aluminium or uPVC, marine-grade fixings, mosquito screens | Salt exposure; insects |
| Exterior lighting | IP65 fittings, stainless fixings | Rain and salt |
| Tiles | Porcelain over ceramic for floors; anti-slip grade on terraces and bathrooms | Humidity, wet floors |
| Doors | Engineered cores rather than solid softwood; sealed edges | Movement with humidity |

These are standard options in every tier; ask for the coastal specification when sending your plans.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Measuring before plaster is complete.
- Leaving tile trims, grout, silicone and door stops off the list.
- Ordering tiles without 8–10 percent cutting allowance.
- Letting the forwarder file the BESC after the vessel has sailed.
- Scheduling the tiler before the container has cleared.
