Decoropic is a China building materials supplier for UAE and Saudi projects, from single villas to hotels and apartment blocks. Foshan is a major production base for tiles, sanitaryware, furniture and lighting, and we buy across those categories for you. ## How delivery works

1. **Drawings and video walk-through.** We measure from your architectural drawings and a video walk-through of the site.
2. **Selection and approval.** You approve samples, finishes and the product list by category.
3. **QC in Foshan.** Goods are checked at our warehouse for quantity, size and visible defects.
4. **Consolidation.** Categories are loaded together into one or more containers, with a packing list and a photo record.
5. **Shipment.** We ship to your port through your nominated forwarder or one we recommend.
6. **Installation support.** Your contractor receives installation drawings, video guides and WhatsApp support.

## Import notes

- Check with your clearing agent which documents your shipment needs, such as a certificate of origin, and whether any must be attested.
- Specify for high heat and strong sunlight: UV-stable outdoor finishes and suitable sealants.
- For coastal projects, choose hardware and fittings suited to salt air.
- Keep invoice, packing list and bill of lading descriptions consistent.
- Ask your consultant early whether the project specification requires particular product test reports.
