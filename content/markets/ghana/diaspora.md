Building a house in Ghana from the UK or USA usually stalls at the finishing stage: choosing kitchens, bathrooms, doors and furniture when you cannot walk through the site or a showroom. Decoropic works with Ghanaians in the UK, USA, Canada, Germany and elsewhere to design, supply and install the interior from a distance. You approve designs over video, pay against agreed milestones, receive photo and video evidence at each stage, and our Accra team looks after the site.

## Key takeaways

- **Zero travel required:** Our Accra team measures the site, you approve 3D designs by video call, and we supervise installation on the ground.
- **Milestone-based payments:** Pay via T/T or L/C tied to verifiable stages (design sign-off, production, Foshan QC, shipping, arrival and handover).
- **Dated photo & video proof:** Receive photos, videos and live video walkthroughs from Foshan warehouse QC through Accra installation.
- **One consolidated container:** Kitchens, wardrobes, bathrooms, doors, tiles, lighting and furniture shipped together to Tema with no minimum order.

## What we handle for diaspora clients

We focus on the finishing and furnishing of the house, which is where most remote projects lose time and money:

- Kitchens and fitted wardrobes
- Bathrooms: sanitaryware, showers, taps and vanities
- Doors and windows
- Tiles where a matched design or consolidation makes sense
- Lighting and furniture packages

If your structure is still going up, we can start design now so materials are ready when the shell is. For the finishing categories, see [doors and windows](/products/doors-windows) and [bathroom and kitchen](/products/bathroom-kitchen).

## Remote design by video

1. **Site measurement** — our Accra team visits, measures every room and records photos and video for you.
2. **Video consultation** — we talk through how you will use the house, your style and your budget level (Functional, Mid-Range or Premium).
3. **3D design** — you receive room designs and a materials list to review in your own time.
4. **Revisions and approval** — changes are made on the drawings before anything is ordered, and you sign off in writing.

Calls are scheduled to suit UK, North American and European time zones.

## Approvals and milestone payments

Paying a large sum up front for a site you cannot see is the main worry for most clients abroad. We split the work into milestones, and each payment is tied to something you can check.

| Milestone | What you receive before or at this stage |
|---|---|
| Design approval | Measured drawings, 3D designs, materials list and quotation |
| Production | Factory confirmation and production photos |
| QC and loading | Photos and video of goods at our Foshan warehouse, packing list |
| Shipping | Container and bill of lading details |
| Arrival and installation | Delivery photos and progress videos from site |
| Handover | Final walkthrough video and snag list closed out |

The exact payment split is agreed in the contract. We accept T/T or L/C. For more detail, read our guide on [paying by milestones when building in Ghana from abroad](/guides/diaspora-building-in-ghana-pay-by-milestones).

## Photo and video evidence at every stage

You should never have to rely on "it's going well". At each stage we send dated photos and video: the measured site, products in the factory, items checked and packed at the warehouse, the container being loaded, delivery to site and installation in progress. You can request a live video walkthrough at any point. ## One container to Tema, one team on site

Everything for the house — kitchen, bathrooms, doors, wardrobes, furniture — is consolidated at our Foshan warehouse and shipped together. There is no minimum order. Our clearing partners handle the port at Tema, and the goods are delivered to your site. Our Ghana team then supervises installation, working with your builder or with fitters we coordinate. If you have family in Ghana who want to be involved, they are welcome to attend site visits and handover.

## FAQ

**Do I need to travel to Ghana during the project?**
No. Design, approvals and progress checks can all be done by video. Many clients visit only for handover.

**Can a relative in Ghana sign off on my behalf?**
Yes, if you confirm this in writing. We still send all approvals and evidence to you directly.

**Can you work with my existing builder?**
Yes. We coordinate dimensions and timing with your builder and supervise the fitting of what we supply.

**How do I pay from the UK or USA?**
By T/T bank transfer or L/C, against the milestones set out in the contract.

**What if something arrives damaged?**
Goods are photographed at loading. Any damage found on delivery is recorded with photos and handled under the contract.
