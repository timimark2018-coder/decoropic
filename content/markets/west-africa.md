## How delivery works

1. **Drawings and video walk-through.** You send drawings; we review the site by video call to confirm sizes and openings.
2. **Product list and quotation.** We prepare a category-by-category list for approval.
3. **Sourcing and QC in Foshan.** Goods are checked for quantity, size and visible defects at our warehouse.
4. **Consolidation.** All categories are loaded into one or more containers, with a packing list and a photo record.
5. **Shipment to your port.** We ship with your nominated forwarder or one we recommend.
6. **Installation support.** Your installers get drawings, video guides and a WhatsApp line to our team.

## Import notes

- Confirm with your clearing agent which documents are required, such as a certificate of origin, before goods are shipped.
- Match descriptions and quantities across the invoice, packing list and bill of lading.
- Specify for heat, humidity and salt air: moisture-resistant cabinet boards, corrosion-resistant hardware and suitable outdoor tiles.
- Transshipment adds time and handling; pack fragile items such as glass and sanitaryware accordingly.
- Agree early who handles clearing and inland haulage at destination.
