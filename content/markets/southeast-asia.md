## How delivery works

1. **Drawings and video walk-through.** We take sizes from your drawings and confirm details on a video call.
2. **Product list and approval.** You approve finishes and quantities category by category.
3. **QC in Foshan.** Goods are checked at our warehouse for quantity, size and visible defects.
4. **Consolidation.** Everything is loaded into one or more containers, with a packing list and a photo record.
5. **Shipment.** We ship through your nominated forwarder or one we recommend, and time loading to your site programme.
6. **Installation support.** Installers get drawings, video guides and WhatsApp support.

## Import notes

- Ask your customs broker which documents apply, such as a certificate of origin for any trade-agreement claim.
- Plan for high humidity: moisture-resistant cabinet boards, suitable adhesives and sealants, and furniture finishes that cope with damp air.
- For beachfront projects, choose hardware and outdoor fittings that tolerate salt air.
- Keep product descriptions consistent across invoice, packing list and bill of lading.
- Short transit means production and QC set the schedule; lock selections early.
