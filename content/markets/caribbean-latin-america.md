## How delivery works

1. **Drawings and video walk-through.** We work from your drawings and confirm openings and room sizes by video.
2. **Product list and approval.** You approve finishes and quantities by category.
3. **QC in Foshan.** Goods are checked for quantity, size and visible defects at our warehouse.
4. **Consolidation and packing.** All categories go into one or more containers, packed for transshipment, with a packing list and photo record.
5. **Shipment.** We ship with your nominated forwarder or one we recommend.
6. **Installation support.** Your installers get drawings, video guides and WhatsApp support across time zones.

## Import notes

- Confirm required documents with your customs broker before shipping, such as a certificate of origin.
- Keep invoice, packing list and bill of lading descriptions consistent.
- For island and coastal sites, specify corrosion-resistant hardware and fittings for salt air.
- Choose finishes for heat, humidity and strong sun, including UV-stable outdoor products.
- Transshipment adds handling; protect glass, sanitaryware and furniture corners with extra packing.
