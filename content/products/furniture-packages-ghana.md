## Fabrics and materials for heat and humidity

Furniture that works in a cool climate does not always work in Accra or Kumasi. We guide clients towards:

- **Breathable upholstery** — performance fabrics, linen blends and cotton-rich weaves rather than heavy velvets, which hold heat.
- **Leather and leather-effect** — easy to clean, but better in air-conditioned rooms; it can feel warm in rooms without AC.
- **Removable covers** where possible, so cushions can be washed.
- **Moisture-resistant boards and sealed edges** on cabinets and wardrobes.
- **Aluminium, powder-coated steel and synthetic rattan** for outdoor furniture, which stand up to sun and rain better than untreated timber.
- **High-density foam** in seat cushions so sofas hold their shape.

## Quality checks before loading

At the Foshan warehouse we open and check furniture before it is packed for export: frame stability, upholstery finish, drawer and door alignment, hardware count and fabric colour against the approved sample. Items are then wrapped, labelled by room and listed on the packing list, so the unloading team in Ghana knows where each box goes.

## Delivery and assembly on site

After clearance at Tema, the container is delivered to your site. Our Ghana team supervises unpacking, assembly and placement room by room, following the layout plan. Beds, wardrobes and dining tables are assembled; sofas and chairs are unwrapped and positioned; packaging is removed. For short-let units, each apartment can be finished and photographed before the next is started.

**Can I furnish just a few rooms?**
Yes. There is no minimum order, and furniture can share a container with other materials.

Yes. We send photos and videos of the actual pieces at the warehouse before loading.

Breathable performance fabrics or cotton and linen blends. Avoid heavy velvet and full leather in those rooms.

Yes, and for short-let units we can include bedding and curtains in each unit set.
