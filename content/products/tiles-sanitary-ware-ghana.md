## Water pressure and fitting compatibility

Many Ghanaian buildings run from roof or ground-level polytanks with a booster pump, so pressure can be low or uneven between floors. That affects what you should buy. Rain shower heads and some thermostatic valves need steady pressure to work properly; for low-pressure systems we specify fittings rated for it, or advise on a pump. Instant electric water heaters are common, and their outlets must suit the shower valve chosen.

We also check thread sizes, waste outlet diameters and the WC outlet type (floor or wall, and the set-out from the wall) against your plumbing drawings before ordering. A mismatch found on site costs far more than one found at the design stage. For a fuller explanation, see our [sanitaryware and bathroom import guide](/guides/sanitaryware-bathroom-import-ghana).

## An honest note on tiles

Ghana now has large local tile production, including Twyford Ghana (Keda Ceramics). For many projects, buying standard floor and wall tiles locally is sensible. We supply project tiles from Foshan when:

- the design calls for a specific look or a matched floor-and-wall series;
- a special format is needed — large slabs, thin porcelain, mosaics or feature tiles;
- the tiles are part of a full-house consolidation, sharing a container with sanitaryware, doors and kitchens.

If a local tile is the better choice, we will say so and design around it.

## Spares and attic stock

On every project we recommend ordering roughly 5% extra tiles from the same batch, plus a small stock of spare fittings: cartridges, flush valves, seat hinges, shower hoses and angle valves. Tile shades vary between production batches, so matching a broken tile years later is difficult without stock from the original run. For hotels and short-let blocks, spare parts on hand mean a room is back in service the same day rather than waiting for the next shipment.

## FAQ

**Can you supply bathrooms for a whole hotel or apartment block?**
Yes. We work from a room schedule and pack by room type or floor so the site team can distribute easily.

**Will Chinese taps fit Ghanaian plumbing?**
Most will, but we check thread sizes, outlets and set-outs against your drawings before ordering rather than assuming.

**Why not buy all the tiles locally?**
Often you should. We supply tiles when the design, format or container consolidation makes it worthwhile.

**How many spare tiles should I order?**
Around 5% extra from the same batch, more for complex layouts with many cuts.

**Do you supply showers for low water pressure?**
Yes. Tell us about your tank, pump and heater, and we specify fittings to suit.
