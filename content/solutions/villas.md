A villa interior materials package from China lets you buy every finish for a private house through one supplier, one contract and one consolidated shipment. Decoropic is the brand of Foshan Home Style Import & Export Co., Ltd., founded in 2011 in Foshan. We measure the house, draw a design around your budget, source each category from factories in Foshan and Guangdong, inspect the goods in our warehouse and ship them together. ## How we deliver

- **Measurement.** In Accra our team visits the villa and measures every room, opening and wet area. For villas outside Ghana we work from your architectural drawings plus a video walk-through.
- **Budget-led design.** Our Foshan design desk prepares the layout and finish selection in 2–3 price tiers, so you can see what changes between tiers before you commit.
- **Sourcing.** We buy each category from the Foshan and Guangdong factories that make it, rather than asking one factory to supply everything.
- **QC and consolidation.** Goods arrive at our Foshan warehouse, where we check quantities, sizes and visible defects before packing them into one shipment.
- **Shipment and clearance.** For Ghana, our clearing partners handle the containers at Tema port. For other countries, we ship through your nominated forwarder.
- **Installation support.** In Ghana our site supervisors guide your installers. Overseas, we provide installation drawings, video guides and a WhatsApp line for questions.
