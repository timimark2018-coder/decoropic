Restaurant fit-out sourcing in China lets you furnish and finish a dining room, bar and washrooms through one supplier instead of several importers. Decoropic, the brand of Foshan Home Style Import & Export Co., Ltd., founded in 2011 in Foshan, designs around your seating plan and budget, buys each category from Foshan and Guangdong factories, checks everything in our warehouse and ships it together. In Ghana our Accra team measures the space and supports installation on site.

- Wall tiles and decorative surfaces for feature walls

## How we deliver

- **Measurement.** In Ghana our Accra team measures the dining area, bar, terrace and washrooms. For restaurants elsewhere we work from your plans and a video walk-through.
- **Budget-led design.** Our Foshan design desk prepares a seating layout and finish selection in 2–3 price tiers, so you can balance cover count, look and cost. Kitchen-facing areas such as service doors and washrooms are included in the same plan.
- **Sourcing.** Furniture, lighting, tiles and sanitaryware come from the Foshan and Guangdong factories that make each category.
- **QC and consolidation.** Our Foshan warehouse checks quantities and visible defects against the order, then packs goods by area: dining room, bar, terrace and washrooms.
- **Shipment and clearance.** Ghana shipments clear at Tema port through our clearing partners. For other markets we ship through your nominated forwarder.
- **Installation support.** Our Ghana site supervisors help your installers fit the bar, lighting and finishes, and check furniture placement against the seating plan before opening. Overseas teams receive drawings, video guides and a WhatsApp line.
