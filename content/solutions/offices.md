Office fit-out furniture and materials from China can be bought as one package covering the workspace, meeting rooms, reception and washrooms. Decoropic, the brand of Foshan Home Style Import & Export Co., Ltd., founded in 2011 in Foshan, plans the layout around your headcount and budget, sources each category from Foshan and Guangdong factories, checks the goods in our warehouse and ships them together. In Ghana our Accra team measures and supervises on site; overseas we work from drawings and video.

## How we deliver

- **Measurement.** In Ghana our Accra team measures the floor plate, columns, windows and service points. For offices elsewhere we use your plans and a video walk-through.
- **Budget-led design.** Our Foshan design desk prepares a furniture layout and finish selection in 2–3 price tiers, based on headcount and the rooms you need.
- **Sourcing.** Office furniture, partitions, lighting and finishes are bought from the Foshan and Guangdong factories that make each category.
- **QC and consolidation.** Goods are checked at our Foshan warehouse for quantity and visible defects, then packed and labelled by zone so your installers can work floor by floor and room by room.
- **Shipment and clearance.** Ghana shipments clear at Tema port through our clearing partners. For other markets we ship through your nominated forwarder.
- **Installation support.** Our Ghana site supervisors help with furniture assembly and placement against the layout. Overseas teams get layout drawings, assembly video guides and a WhatsApp line.
