Working with a hotel FF&E supplier in China makes sense when you want guestrooms, public areas and back-of-house supplied from one source. Decoropic is the brand of Foshan Home Style Import & Export Co., Ltd., founded in 2011 in Foshan. We source furniture, fixtures and equipment together with the hard finishes they sit on: tiles, doors, bathrooms and lighting. Everything is checked in our Foshan warehouse and shipped together, with our Accra team on site for projects in Ghana.

- Guestroom seating: lounge chairs, desk chairs and sofas
- Lobby and lounge furniture, including reception counters
- Restaurant and bar tables, chairs and banquettes
- Guestroom and corridor lighting, plus feature pendants for public areas
- Bathroom sets: vanities, toilets, showers, mixers, mirrors and accessories
- Floor and wall tiles for rooms, bathrooms, corridors and lobby
- Guestroom doors, frames and hardware
- Curtains, blackout blinds and sheers
- Outdoor furniture for pool and terrace areas
- Room-by-room packing and labelling to match your room schedule

## How we deliver

- **Measurement.** In Ghana our Accra team measures typical rooms, suites and public areas. For hotels elsewhere we work from your interior drawings and a video walk-through.
- **Budget-led design.** Our Foshan design desk develops the FF&E schedule in 2–3 price tiers, and can work to an operator's brand standards if you provide them.
- **Sourcing.** Furniture, lighting, sanitaryware and finishes come from the Foshan and Guangdong factories that specialise in each category.
- **QC and consolidation.** We check items at our Foshan warehouse against the FF&E schedule, then pack by floor or room type so your site team can issue goods in order.
- **Shipment and clearance.** Ghana shipments clear at Tema port through our clearing partners. For other countries we ship through your nominated forwarder.
- **Installation support.** Our Ghana site supervisors help coordinate furniture placement and fixture installation, starting with the sample room. Overseas projects get drawings, video guides and a WhatsApp line.
