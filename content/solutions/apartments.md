Apartment fit-out materials from China work best when every unit is specified once and supplied as a repeatable pack. Decoropic, the brand of Foshan Home Style Import & Export Co., Ltd., founded in 2011 in Foshan, helps developers and contractors define a standard unit specification, source it across categories, check it in our Foshan warehouse and ship it in planned batches. Our Accra team measures sites in Ghana; for projects elsewhere we work from drawings and video walk-throughs.

## What's in the package

- Floor tiles for living areas and bedrooms, specified per unit type
- Bathroom wall and floor tiles, plus kitchen backsplash tiles
- Corridor, lobby and stair tiles for common areas
- Apartment entrance doors with locks, and interior doors with frames
- Aluminium windows and balcony sliding doors
- Kitchen cabinets and worktops in standard module sizes
- Sanitaryware: toilets, basins, shower sets and mixers per bathroom
- Bedroom wardrobes
- Ceiling lights and downlights per room, plus common-area lighting
- Optional loose furniture packs for furnished or serviced units
- Unit-by-unit packing lists so each floor can be issued separately on site

## How we deliver

- **Measurement.** In Ghana our Accra team measures a sample of each unit type and the common areas. For overseas blocks we work from your floor plans plus a video walk-through of a completed shell.
- **Budget-led design.** The Foshan design desk prepares the unit specification in 2–3 price tiers, so you can match the finish level to your sales price or rent.
- **Sourcing.** Each category comes from the Foshan or Guangdong factory that makes it, with the same specification repeated across all units.
- **QC and consolidation.** Our Foshan warehouse checks quantities and visible defects against the unit schedule, then packs goods by unit type or floor.
- **Shipment and clearance.** Ghana shipments are cleared at Tema port through our clearing partners. For other markets we ship through your nominated forwarder. Large blocks can be shipped in phases to match your construction programme.
- **Installation support.** Our Ghana site supervisors help your installers fit the first units correctly. Overseas teams receive drawings, video guides and a WhatsApp line.
