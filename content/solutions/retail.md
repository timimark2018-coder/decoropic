Showroom and retail fit-out materials from China can cover everything a shop needs to open: display fixtures, counters, lighting, flooring and the finishes around them. Decoropic, the brand of Foshan Home Style Import & Export Co., Ltd., founded in 2011 in Foshan, plans the layout around your products and budget, sources each category from Foshan and Guangdong factories, checks it in our warehouse and ships it together. In Ghana our Accra team measures and supports installation on site.

## How we deliver

- **Measurement.** In Ghana our Accra team measures the shop or showroom, including the storefront, ceiling height and power points. For stores elsewhere we work from your plans and a video walk-through.
- **Budget-led design.** Our Foshan design desk prepares a layout and display plan in 2–3 price tiers, built around the products you sell.
- **Sourcing.** Display fixtures, lighting, flooring and glass are bought from the Foshan and Guangdong factories that make each category.
- **QC and consolidation.** Our Foshan warehouse checks quantities and visible defects, then packs fixtures by zone, with labels matching the layout drawing, so your installers can assemble the shop in order.
- **Shipment and clearance.** Ghana shipments clear at Tema port through our clearing partners. For other markets we ship through your nominated forwarder.
- **Installation support.** Our Ghana site supervisors help your team assemble and position fixtures. Overseas stores receive layout drawings, video guides and a WhatsApp line.
